# Reflective Question Bank

Section 5 of the ritual uses 2-3 reflective questions. Asking the same three every week makes the reflection feel routine and produces shallower answers over time.

Use the **default set** most weeks. Swap in 1-2 from the **alternative banks** every 3-4 weeks to keep the reflection alive. Tailor based on what came up earlier in the review — if Section 4 surfaced heavy stuck patterns, lean into questions about energy or fear. If goal coverage was lopsided, lean into questions about intent.

## Default set (use most weeks)

1. **What worked well this week?** ("Worked" = decisions, habits, environments, energies — not just tasks completed.)
2. **What didn't work?** ("Didn't work" = systems, schedules, expectations that produced friction.)
3. **What's one thing I want different next week?**

## Alternative bank — energy and pace

- What time of day did I do my best work this week?
- When did I feel drained, and was there a pattern in what came before it?
- Was there a day I had energy and didn't spend it well? What happened?
- Where did I push when I should have rested? Where did I rest when I should have pushed?

## Alternative bank — relationships and people

- Who did I connect with that mattered? Who slipped that shouldn't have?
- Was there a conversation I avoided this week that's still sitting with me?
- Did anyone help me unexpectedly? Did I thank them?
- Was there a moment of conflict or tension — what did I learn?

## Alternative bank — meaning and direction

- What did I do this week that felt like the work I actually want to be doing?
- What did I do that I'd rather not have done?
- Is there something I keep meaning to start that I haven't?
- Looking at this week in 6 months, what would I be glad I did? What would I regret?

## Alternative bank — friction and obstacles

- What's the biggest source of friction in my current setup?
- Where am I working around a problem instead of solving it?
- What tool, habit, or environment change would have made this week easier?
- What did I try that didn't work — and what's the lesson?

## Alternative bank — wins and gratitude

- What's one thing I'm proud of from this week, even if small?
- What surprised me — a moment of unexpected ease or insight?
- Who or what am I grateful for from this week?
- What did I get right that I might have missed in the moment?

## Choosing questions for a specific week

Look at what came up in Sections 2-4:

- **MITs heavily missed** → Default Q2 + one from the friction bank
- **Goal areas heavily neglected** → Default Q3 + one from the meaning bank
- **Many unblock sessions** → Default Q1 (focus on what worked) + one from the energy bank
- **Light week, mostly went well** → Default Q1 + one from the wins/gratitude bank
- **First review ever** → Stick to the default set

When in doubt: default set. Variety is good, but consistency lets patterns emerge across reviews.
