## Template usage notes (for the skill — not part of the output)

- Replace ALL bracketed placeholders.
- The frontmatter (decision/date/review_date/status) makes the entry machine-readable for the review-due check. Don't skip it.
- "Status" values: Open (default), Reviewed (after review), Cancelled (rare — if the decision was abandoned before review).
- For "Anticipated outcomes" likelihoods: rough is fine. "60%" or "low/medium/high" or "~1 in 3" all work. The point is forcing self-honesty, not calibrated probability.
- For "What would change my mind": this is where most journal entries get thin. Push the user to be specific. "If the customer doesn't sign within 30 days" beats "if things go poorly."
- Capture the user's verbatim language. Don't translate "the Acme deal" to "the customer engagement."
- The review section is appended by Mode B (Review a past decision). The original entry stays unchanged; the review is added after.
