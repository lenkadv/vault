## Template usage notes (for the skill — not part of the output)

- This section is APPENDED to the original entry. Don't replace anything in the original — the full arc should be visible in one file.
- The status line in the frontmatter at the top of the original entry should be changed to `Reviewed` once this section is added. Also add the review date to the frontmatter as `reviewed_date: YYYY-MM-DD`.
- The "Was the decision good?" question is THE central question of the review. Push the user toward an honest answer. If they hedge ("kind of"), follow up: "given specifically what you knew on [decision date], setting aside what you know now — good call or not?"
- The "Lesson" section is the long-term value. Don't let it become situation-specific. The lesson should be portable to future, different decisions.
- Keep the review tight. The point is to learn, not to write an essay. 200-400 words for the whole review section is right.
