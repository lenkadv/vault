# Add to brain

> Drop anything here that the marketing team could learn from. GrowOS reads
> what you put in this folder and files it into the right place in your
> brain. This README explains the folder. You do not fill it in.

## What to drop here

- Old emails and newsletters
- Call or video transcripts
- PDFs
- A sales page export
- Past posts
- Screenshots
- Videos

Anything else that shows how you talk to customers, or what you have said
before, belongs here too.

## Keep secrets out

Never drop a password, API key, token, or credential export in this folder.
Secrets have one home: the business's private `.env` file.

## How to get it read

During setup, just add files while GrowOS is working. When you are ready,
say "continue" and it moves on.

Any other time, drop files in and say "check the folder." GrowOS reads what
is new and files it away.

## Your originals are safe

Nothing you drop here is ever deleted. Once a file is read, GrowOS moves it to
its proper spot in your brain folder and adds a line to `filed-log.md` right
here, saying what went where. That log is your receipt. Empty means everything
was processed and moved; the log records anything GrowOS could not read
itself (like a video), so nothing is silently skipped.
