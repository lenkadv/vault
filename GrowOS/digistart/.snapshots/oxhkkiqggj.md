## The promise: buyer, outcome, boundary

**Buyer:** a solo businesswoman or sole trader (lecturer, coach, adviser) who already tried AI and uses it to get answers.
**Outcome:** AI does a piece of her work for her, not only answers her questions. *Source:* Lenka, 2026-10-14 (decisions.md) and the owners' wording "AI za mě udělá kus práce" (audience.md, unchecked).
**Boundary:** four live meetings, built with the participants. It is not for someone who has never tried AI, and not for someone who wants to program.

## Who it is for, and who it is not

**For:** solo businesswomen and sole traders who already tried AI (typically ChatGPT) and use it as a smarter search engine. *Source:* audience.md, rough/unchecked.
**Not for, each with a reason (audience.md):**
- someone who never tried AI: the course skips "open ChatGPT" on purpose, so she would be left behind;
- someone who wants to program or automate technically: this is not an IT course;
- someone who is still deciding what to sell: the course assumes an offer and customers exist;
- teachers and firms: a different offer. Firms are the separate company workshop.
