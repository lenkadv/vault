## The promise: buyer, outcome, boundary

**Buyer:** women who already tried AI and want it to do real work in their own work. The core group is solo businesswomen and sole traders (audience.md), but the ad and page wording does not narrow it to "podnikatelky": two women will attract other women too (Lenka, 2026-10-14).
**Outcome:** AI does a piece of her work for her, not only answers her questions, and she is guided step by step through building an AI system of her own. *Source:* Lenka, 2026-10-14 (decisions.md) and the owners' wording "AI za mě udělá kus práce" (audience.md, unchecked).
**Boundary:** four live meetings, led step by step. It is not for someone who has never tried AI, and not for someone who wants to program.

Draft page wording (Czech, generic feminine, no age, no puffed-up words). The benefit of the pilot is said as a benefit: the women influence what comes next.

> **AI nemusí jen odpovídat. Může za vás odvést kus práce.**

AI umí víc: uklidit složky na disku, napsat koncept odpovědi vaším stylem, a dokonce postavit webovou stránku a dát ji na internet, což jsme zvládly i my dvě. *Check:* the examples (files, an e-mail draft in her style, a page put online) say what AI can do. They are shown by the test run with Štěpánka and by what Lenka and Štěpánka built. ## Who it is for, and who it is not

**For:** women, mostly solo businesswomen and sole traders, who already tried AI (typically ChatGPT) and use it as a smarter search engine. *Source:* audience.md, rough/unchecked.
**Not for, each with a reason (audience.md):**
- someone who never tried AI: the course skips "open ChatGPT" on purpose, so she would be left behind;
- someone who wants to program or automate technically: this is not an IT course;
- someone who is still deciding what to sell: the course assumes an offer and customers exist;
- teachers and firms: a different offer. Firms are the separate company workshop.
