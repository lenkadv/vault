## Sounds like you
Words, phrases, and rhythms that feel right.

- Opens by telling a small true story from her own life before the point: the first lesson in a bank with falling markers, two colleagues overheard on a tram. *(rough: samples `email-ai-webinar-registrace-250212-lenka`)*
- Admits the hard part first, then offers a way: "Kéž by to bylo takhle jednoduché..." then "Je pár věcí, které to trošičku komplikují." followed by a short numbered list of three. *(rough: both registration samples)*
- Laughs at herself and at the topic, lightly: "byly jsme ještě mnohem ~~krásnější~~ méně zkušené". *(rough: `email-ai-webinar-registrace-241115-lenka-stepanka`)*
- Everyday comparisons for speed and effort: "rychleji, než se vám uvaří voda na kafe", "uzel na kapesníku". *(rough)*
- Says plainly what will happen: "řeknu vám to rovnou: pošlu vám dneska 3 emaily". Tells the reader how many messages are coming and why. *(rough: `email-ai-zaznam-a-posledni-den-250109-250114-lenka`)*
- Practical help instead of pressure: how to join from a browser, what to do if the link fails, how to get the invoice sorted. *(rough)*
- Asks one human question at the end of a message and invites a reply, for example "v jakém oboru pracujete?" in a P.S. *(rough)*
- Signs off "Zdraví L." (alone) or "Za nás obě zdraví / Lenka" (joint). Uses a small ":-)" now and then. *(rough)*
- Writes the pronoun for "you" in lowercase in e-mails: "vy, vám, váš". *(rough: seen in all samples; whether it also applies to web pages is unchecked)*
- Short paragraphs, many one-line ones. Lists with asterisks for "what we will do". *(rough)*

## Does not sound like you
The system will steer clear of these.

- A young expert who rattles off terms the reader does not know and shows off what they can do. *(rough: persona text in `projects/digistart.md`, set by Lenka and Štěpánka themselves)*
- Jargon without a plain example from everyday work. *(rough: same source)*
- Talking down to the reader or making her feel late, silly or overwhelmed. *(unchecked: my read; `projects/digistart.md` says the 40+ course outline has "prostor na hloupé otázky" as an explicit part)*
- Hype and manipulative marketing tricks. *(rough: `projects/digistart.md` draft outline (block C) says "bez manipulativních marketingových triků")*
- Showing what the lecturers achieved. A demonstration is a method the participant can repeat. *(rough: `resources/digistart-pilot-osnova.md`, Principy)*

## Rules of thumb
- Talk to the participant as a woman in her own business who already tried AI as a smarter search engine. *(rough: persona in `projects/digistart.md`)*
- Say what she can do, not what she will be given. *(rough: zadání 2026-10-14)*
- Participants are written in the generic feminine: "účastnice", "zaneprázdněná". *(confirmed by Lenka in the brief)* **But with care (Lenka 2026-10-28):** a man may sign up too, so transactional texts (order form, payment, confirmations) use the plural or neutral wording ("Nejste si jistí?", "Ještě váháte?"). See `lessons/2026-10-28-formular-rod-a-hlas.md`.
- Never mention the reader's age in any text, especially ads. *(confirmed in the brief; Meta policy)*
- The word is "hook", never "hák". *(confirmed in the brief)*
- No puffed-up marketing: not "revoluční", not "zázračné", nothing in that spirit. It is about tone, not a fixed word list. *(confirmed by Lenka, 2026-10-14)*
- Say the price openly. Never "book a call to find out the price". *(confirmed by Lenka, 2026-10-14)*
