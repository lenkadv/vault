## 2026-10-14: the price is shown openly
**Decided (Lenka, with Štěpánka, said in the setup chat):** the price of 15 000 Kč is not hidden behind a consultation. Common sales advice says not to give the price in advance and to reveal it on a booked call. DigiStart will not use it.

## 2026-10-14: what the opening message says
**Decided (Lenka, in the setup chat):** the message at the start of the ad and the page is about everyday needs of a small business owner, but one level above "use AI for your daily work". People already think of AI as something they ask for an e-mail, a plan, a text or a table, and then copy the answer around themselves. DigiStart shows the next step: AI can do the work itself. The wow is that it can build a website and put it online itself. This difference must be said right at the start.
**Why:** "Use AI for your daily work" is too little; participants already have that picture. The step up is AI working for the small business owner, not the owner working for AI.
**Open:** what exactly can be promised about the website (built by AI, put online by AI) needs a check against what the course really delivers before any ad says it. See [[audience]] and the offer once written.
