# Lessons

> This folder is where the system keeps what it has learned about your taste: the
> corrections you make, the patterns you like, the things you always change. It
> fills up on its own as you review work. You do not have to write here, though you
> are welcome to.

## What this is for

Every time you fix a draft or say "not like that, like this," there is a lesson in
it. Instead of making the same miss twice, the system writes the lesson down here
and reads it before the next piece of work. Over time, this is a big part of why
GrowOS starts to feel like it just gets you.

## What lands here

- Corrections that come up more than once (a word you always cut, a tone you always
  soften).
- Preferences you have stated (how long posts should run, how you like to open).
- Small rules that keep the work sounding like you.

You can read these any time to see what the system has picked up. If a lesson is
wrong or out of date, tell the system, or edit the file. It is yours.
