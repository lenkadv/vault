2026-10-28 - Lenka edited my first texts for the SimpleShop order form (`work/pages/digistart-simpleshop-formular.md`; my version in commit "DigiStart objednávkový formulář SimpleShop – návrh", hers in "Lenčiny úpravy textů"). She also said I had not studied her voice. What to do next time:

- **Grammatical gender is chosen with care, not by a fixed rule.** The audience is women, but a man may sign up too. Where the text speaks to "whoever is buying" (form, payment, transactional pages), use the plural or a neutral wording: "Nejste si jistí?", "Pokud jste zvolili převod". Neutral phrasing that needs no gender is even better ("Ještě váháte?"). Feminine stays where the text paints the target reader (ads, sales page stories). Never apply "generic feminine everywhere" mechanically.
- **Explain the consequence, plainly.** My "Prosíme o správný variabilní symbol, jinak platbu nespárujeme" became "Při zadávání platby dejte pozor na variabilní symbol – když ho netrefíte, nedozvíme se o vaší platbě včas." Everyday words, the reader's point of view, not office language ("spárovat").
- **Warm, a little celebratory where it fits.** "Máte místo v kurzu DigiStart." became "Místo v kurzu DigiStart je vaše!" Questions phrased as an invitation: "Potřebujete se na něco zeptat? Napište na: …"
- **One choice, not a menu, when the answer is obvious.** She kept one name ("Kurz DigiStart – 4 pondělí od 23. 11. 2026") and one button ("Závazně se přihlásit a zaplatit").
- **Even short transactional copy is written in her voice from the start:** read `brain/voice.md`, her samples and the newest lessons first (especially `2026-10-28-proc-claude-sila-ne-pohodli.md`: sell the trained assistant and saved time, not the chat), reuse wording she already approved, then write. Do not draft neutral text and leave the voice for her to add.
