## What we sell and who it is for
**The DigiStart pilot, 15 000 Kč**: four live weekly meetings, built step by step with the participants, where AI does real work for a solo businesswoman. It is for solo businesswomen and sole traders (lecturers, coaches, advisers) who already tried AI and are disappointed that it stayed at asking questions. Details in [[business]] and [[audience]]. The audience picture is thin and **unchecked**: it comes from the two lecturers' own view, not from customers.

## The weekly rhythm
Lenka's time is about **10 hours a week** (2026-10-14). She can arrange it on weekends too. The 10 hours are her time only: Štěpánka can work on it as well, so the total can be higher.

Lenka does not want to commit to exact times. The reason: it is uncertain how long each step takes and exactly what each result should look like. So the plan works as an **ideal picture to cut down from, or to shift in time**, not as fixed slots.

- One review session a week for the queue (page, ads, posts).
- The rest goes along the critical path of the campaign plan: offer, page, Facebook page, measuring, ad, consultation booking, test of the whole path.
- **The ideal picture** is the campaign plan's: page, Facebook page, ad round, booking, measuring, test, and video. Its own estimate is 22 to 28 hours of Lenka's time without video, for 19 to 30 October. Counted against 10 hours a week, that does not fit by itself. Lenka (2026-10-14): not everything has to be known and written down firmly now, because it will still develop. So there is no fixed decision to cut or move; this is checked as the work shows how long things really take.

## This month's themes
(From today to mid-November.)

- **Say the step up.** AI can really work for a small business owner, not only answer questions. The first words of the ad and page say this. What exactly can be promised about a website built and put online by AI is still to be checked (see [[decisions]]).
- **Make a pilot with no references feel safe.** Two lecturers with faces and names, an open price, a course built with the participants. No invented proof; the first real words will come from the consultations.
- **Make the next step easy.** Book a consultation or buy straight away, no hidden price.
