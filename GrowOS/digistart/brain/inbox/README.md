# Inbox

> This folder is your catch-all. Drop anything here: a half-formed idea, a story you
> want to remember, a screenshot of a nice review, a note to yourself. The system
> sorts it into the right place later so nothing gets lost. You do not have to keep
> this tidy.

## How to use it

When a thought or a piece of proof shows up and you do not have time to file it,
just drop it here. A quick note, a pasted message, a photo, whatever. Getting it
out of your head and into the inbox is the whole job.

Later, the system can go through the inbox and file each thing where it belongs:
- a customer's kind words move to `proof/`
- a good story moves to `stories/`
- an idea for a post becomes work in the queue
- a preference becomes a lesson

## Good things to drop here

- A review, DM, or email worth keeping as proof.
- A story or moment before you forget it.
- An idea for a post, email, or campaign.
- Anything you are not sure what to do with yet.

Nothing here is required. An empty inbox is a fine inbox.
