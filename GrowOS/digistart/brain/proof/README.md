# Proof

> This folder holds your real proof: testimonials, results, and kind words from
> real customers. The system pulls from here whenever it needs to back up a claim,
> so nothing gets made up. One file per quote.
>
> Most of this README explains how the folder works and stays as it is. **One
> section is yours to keep up:** "The gaps worth closing" at the bottom.

## The rule that makes proof safe

The system will only ever use proof that lives in this folder, exactly as it was
said. It never invents a testimonial or a result. If it needs proof it does not
have, it leaves a blank for you instead of making something up.

## How to add proof

Copy `_template.md`, give it a short name (like `sarah-time-saved.md`), and fill
it in. The fields are:

- **the quote**: paste it word for word, never reworded
- **name**: who said it
- **source**: where it came from (an email reply, a review, a message)
- **date**: when
- **approval**: starts as `pending`

## Permission comes before use

A nice quote is not the same as permission to publish it. Every new entry starts
with `approval: pending`. It stays that way until the person has clearly said yes
to you using it publicly. Only you flip it to `approved`. The system will flag
proof that looks usable, but it never grants the permission for you.

If a screenshot, photo, or video came with the quote, save it next to the entry
and note where it is, so the proof travels with its receipt.

## Picking the right proof

Not every quote fits every claim. The system picks by matching what the proof
actually says to what the work is claiming, and prefers, in this order:

1. **Proof that speaks to the exact claim.** A quote about saving time backs a
   time claim. It does not back a money claim, however glowing it is.
2. **Approved over pending.** Pending proof is never used publicly at all.
3. **Named and specific over anonymous and vague.** "Cut my invoicing from
   Sunday nights to twenty minutes — Sam, bookkeeper" beats "Great service!"
4. **Recent over old**, unless the older one is a much better fit.
5. **One not used lately**, so the same three quotes do not appear everywhere.

When nothing here fits the claim, the system writes `[PLACEHOLDER]` and tells you
what proof is missing. It never stretches a quote to cover something it does not
say, and it never uses a pending one to fill a gap.

## The gaps worth closing

**This is the section you maintain.** Everything above explains the folder; this
list is content.

Proof is the most under-collected thing in almost every business, and it is
load-bearing for every claim GrowOS makes on your behalf. It is worth knowing
which claims you currently cannot back.

Keep a short list here of proof you wish you had. When the system spots a gap
while writing, it adds to this list rather than inventing something.

- [PLACEHOLDER: a claim you would like to make but have no proof for yet]
- [PLACEHOLDER: another, or delete this line]

The easiest way to close a gap: when a customer says something good, ask right
then whether you can quote them. Later almost never happens.
