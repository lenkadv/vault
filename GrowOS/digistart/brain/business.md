## What makes us different
In Lenka's words (setup chat, 2026-10-14), shortened, not word for word:

- A one-day course cannot be enough. Working with AI the way DigiStart teaches it is a sequence of steps that a person has to try and absorb.
- DigiStart builds step by step: a task, then room to try it, then the next step builds on it. Example from the plan: first, order on the disk, while the assistant gets to know the documents. Next, the voice profiles, which can draw on those documents. This cannot be done in one day without a mountain of information.
- Participants come with their own laptop, may use a desktop, and may not have passwords at hand. A planned workshop with essential basics in between handles that. A one-day, in-person course does not.
- No time spent on "open ChatGPT" basics: DigiStart aims at women who already tried AI.
- When people are at different levels, a single day fails: whoever falls behind takes in nothing more. Ongoing support, trying things during the workshop and direct contact with the lecturers are better.

- A package of tools, or a video that shows what AI can do, is not enough. Without a guide who says "today we try this" and leads you through the steps, it stays painful. DigiStart is that guide, and the steps are prepared in advance. Lenka's own story and the test run with Štěpánka, in her words: [[stories/2026-10-14-bez-pruvodce-je-to-bolestne]] (internal for now). — **confirmed** (Lenka, 2026-10-14)

Lenka said she has **not seen** other one-day courses, so what she says about them is her belief, not a checked fact. Marketing must not describe competitors as "nalejvárna" or say what their courses lack. The research found that Umeligence and Digitari sell one-day courses (see [[competitors]], **unchecked**).

The message that comes first (Lenka, 2026-10-14, see [[decisions]]): not only "use AI for your daily work", which people already picture, but a level above: AI can really work for the small business owner. A website is one example, and it is something the two lecturers did: they built a page with AI and put it online. It is a real pain for solo businesswomen: sites that look poor, are hard to change, and the owner does not know what to write. Lenka does not want the offer to dwell on websites.
Open: whether the course itself includes building one. Until the content of the meetings says so, the website is only an example of what AI can do.
