# 10 Sentences — Caravaggio: The Supper at Emmaus

*Version A — general*

1. Caravaggio was an Italian painter.
2. He lived from 1571 to 1610.
3. He was famous for his dramatic use of light and shadow.
4. This technique is called chiaroscuro.
5. The Supper at Emmaus was painted in 1601.
6. It shows the moment when two men recognize Jesus after his resurrection.
7. The figures look like ordinary people from the street.
8. The light comes from one side and makes the scene very dramatic.
9. You can see the surprise on the men's faces.
10. Caravaggio changed the way artists painted people.
