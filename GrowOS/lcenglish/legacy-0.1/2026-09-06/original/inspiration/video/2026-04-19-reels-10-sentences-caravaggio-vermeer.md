# 10 Sentences — Reels Format

Two scripts for the weekly "10 sentences about an artist and painting" format.
Each sentence is standalone — read slowly, one by one.

---

## Caravaggio — The Supper at Emmaus

*Version A — general*

1. Caravaggio was an Italian painter.
2. He lived from 1571 to 1610.
3. He was famous for his dramatic use of light and shadow.
4. This technique is called chiaroscuro.
5. The Supper at Emmaus was painted in 1601.
6. It shows the moment when two men recognize Jesus after his resurrection.
7. The figures look like ordinary people from the street.
8. The light comes from one side and makes the scene very dramatic.
9. You can see the surprise on the men's faces.
10. Caravaggio changed the way artists painted people.

*Version B — grammar focus: Present Continuous*

1. Caravaggio was an Italian painter.
2. He lived from 1571 to 1610.
3. He was famous for his dramatic use of light and shadow.
4. In the painting, Jesus is sitting at a table.
5. Two men are looking at him in surprise.
6. One man is stretching out his arms.
7. Another man is gripping the arms of his chair.
8. A servant is standing next to the table.
9. The light is falling on Jesus's face.
10. Everyone is reacting to the same moment.

## Vermeer — The Milkmaid

*Version A — general*

1. Johannes Vermeer was a Dutch painter.
2. He lived from 1632 to 1675.
3. He painted quiet scenes from everyday life.
4. The Milkmaid was painted around 1658.
5. It shows a woman pouring milk in a kitchen.
6. The light comes through a window on the left.
7. Everything in the painting looks very still and calm.
8. Vermeer used tiny dots of paint to create texture.
9. The painting is not very big — about 45 by 41 centimetres.
10. Today, it hangs in the Rijksmuseum in Amsterdam.

*Version B — grammar focus: There is / There are*

1. Johannes Vermeer was a Dutch painter.
2. He lived from 1632 to 1675.
3. He painted quiet scenes from everyday life.
4. In the painting, there is a woman standing at a table.
5. There is a jug in her hands.
6. There is a bowl on the table.
7. There is bread next to the bowl.
8. There is a window on the left side of the room.
9. There is soft light coming through the glass.
10. There are no other people in the room.
