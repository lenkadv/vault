## English Hacker

**Who they are:** online English course business run by Markéta Růžičková, built
around her own personal story of learning to speak.

**Positioning and promise (their claim):** teaches people to speak English
"zábavně a efektivně" (in a fun, effective way) — their own framing, aimed
at people who find English intimidating.

**Offers and visible pricing:** English Hacker Premium, 2.790 Kč, lifetime
access — per englishhackerpremium.cz, checked 2026-09-14.

**Channels visibly in use:** own site with a large free article/blog section
("Recenze", "Online kurz angličtiny" categories), an active author presence.

**What their customers praise (quoted, found via search, medium confidence —
more than one independent mention, not read directly on a review platform):**
- "Markéta je skvělá učitelka, její pojetí učení angličtiny je zkrátka jiné —
  jednoduché, zábavné a efektivní."
- "Jako aktivní student kurzu English Hacker lze tento projekt pouze doporučit,
  propracovanost lekcí a procvičovacích nahrávek je na velmi vysoké úrovni."

**What their customers/reviewers name as a weakness (low confidence — one
third-party review roundup, not a direct customer complaint):** no mobile app,
no offline mode; materials are PDF/Word/MP3 downloads you fill in by hand, not
an interactive online tool.

**Where LCEnglish is genuinely different (evidence-based):** LCEnglish's
Shadowloop app is an interactive practice tool used across all three courses —
the exact gap the English Hacker review roundup names (no online fill-in,
download-and-do-it-yourself materials). This is checked against English Hacker
specifically, not a claim about the whole market.

## Broňa.cz (Bronislav Sobotka)

**Who they are:** solo English teacher based in Brno, one of the best-known
English teaching personalities in Czech media — much larger public profile
than the other names here.

**Positioning and promise (their claim):** fear of mistakes, not English
itself, is the real obstacle — teaches people to get over that fear and speak.

**Offers and visible pricing:** "Kompletní angličtina s Broňou" — 290 lessons,
3.490 Kč, unlimited access (per earlier search of brona.cz/online-kurzy).

**Channels visibly in use:** YouTube — **188,000 subscribers** (per an em.muni.cz
alumni profile piece, 2026); own site; listed on third-party course
marketplaces (Seduo.cz, LearnTube.cz); regular Czech media coverage (Český
rozhlas Brno, Moje psychologie, MUNI alumni magazine).

**What their customers praise (medium confidence, from Seduo.cz's own rating
summary, not individually quoted):** Seduo.cz lists 98 ratings for his course,
80 of them five stars.

**What their customers complain about:** not found this pass — no individual
negative review text surfaced in search. Say so plainly rather than guessing.

**Where LCEnglish is genuinely different:** no evidence-based difference found
this pass — nothing in what was read names a specific gap in Broňa's offer that
LCEnglish fills. Honest gap, not filled with a guess. Worth naming instead: this
is by far the biggest and most visible name on this list (188k YouTube vs.
LCEnglish's dormant channel), and any positioning against him has to reckon
with that reach gap directly, not just a methodology difference.

vocabulary, listening, exam prep, forum — not a single paid course.

**Positioning and promise (their claim):** "Angličtina na internetu zdarma" —
free English on the internet, made by experienced teachers.

**Offers and visible pricing:** the core site is free; homepage now also

**Channels visibly in use:** own site with a large partner network shown on

**What their customers praise / complain about:** not checked this pass (site

were already well established in the old file; this pass only re-confirmed it

## KubovaEnglish

**Who they are:** solo creator ("Kuba"), online English courses, self-described
as "nejznámější český učitel angličtiny" (the most famous Czech English
teacher) — their own claim, not verified.

**Positioning and promise (their claim):** step-by-step English in 30 days;
individual topic courses (pronunciation, CZENGLISH mistakes, exam prep).

**Offers and visible pricing:** individual courses roughly 2.000–3.000 Kč, with
bundle discounts (per search snippets of kubovaenglish.cz course pages, not
independently confirmed on the pricing page itself).

**Channels visibly in use / customer praise / complaints:** not found this
pass beyond the course listing.

**Owner's own read (recorded, not independently verified):** aimed at a
different, likely younger or more casual audience than LCEnglish's skeptical
40–65 self-learners — tone is much more irreverent/crude, per the owner.
