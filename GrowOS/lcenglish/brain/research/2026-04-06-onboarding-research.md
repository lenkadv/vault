## Audience Research

**Primary pain points (verbatim from Czech forums/communities):**
- "Nerozumím rodilým mluvčím."
- "Bojím se mluvit anglicky."
- "Gramatiku znám, ale mluvit neumím."
- "Učení mě nebaví, jde to pomalu."
- Foreign Language Anxiety is a documented phenomenon among Czech learners.
- Specific Czech cultural trait: fear of embarrassment from mistakes, worry about being laughed at by peers.

**Awareness level:** Solution-aware but skeptical. They've tried methods before. Key objection: "why would this be different?"

**Where they hang out:** Help for English forums, Facebook language groups, YouTube
