## 1. Who they are (segments seen in evidence)

- **Adult self-learners who have tried before and doubt themselves.** Directly
  named age as a reason to doubt ("je mi 48 let, moc jsem nedoufala, že se
  něco naučím" — Romana Svobodová, 10x ENGLISH testimonial, 2020). **medium.**
- **People who weren't actively looking for a course.** At least one entry
  point was not "shopping for English lessons" at all, but discovering Lenka's
  personality/enthusiasm first ("Na Váš kurz jsem přišla náhodou... Navnadil
  mě Váš sympatický projev" — Marie D., HELE testimonial, 2021). **low** (one
  source) — worth watching for, not yet a pattern.

## 2. Core problem, in their own words

- Not understanding spoken English / not being able to speak despite years of
  studying: "Hledal jsem někoho, kdo mi pomůže se opravdu rozmluvit" (Petr
  Titěra, 10x ENGLISH testimonial). **medium** — echoed by Romana Svobodová
  ("dodat si odvahu a začít mluvit") and Dáša Nastulczyková ("nejúžasnější byl
  pocit, že opravdu mluvím plynule anglicky").

## 3. Emotional drivers (each backed by quotes)

- **Fear of being incapable, specifically tied to age or past failure:**
  "co mi Váš kurz vzal? Pocit, že na to nemám!!!!!" (Daniela Tájková, HELE
  testimonial, 2019). **medium** — same shape as Romana Svobodová's "moc jsem
  nedoufala."
- **Relief/surprise at a felt sense of fluency:** "že to není něco, co bych
  nedokázala, že to není neřešitelný problém" (Dáša Nastulczyková, 10x ENGLISH
  landing page quote). **low** (one source).

## 4. Past attempts that failed them, in their words

- "To se zatím žádné výukové metodě nepodařilo" [rozumět zvukům jazyka při
  poslechu] — Vít Bělík, HELE testimonial, 2021. **low**, but sharp and
  specific: names listening comprehension as the thing every prior method
  failed at for him.
- "Doposud jsem netušila, kolik existuje možností... jak moc se kurz liší od
  toho, co všichni známe" — Ivana Lafková, 10x ENGLISH testimonial, 2020.
  Implies prior exposure to generic/interchangeable courses. **low.**

Business.md already states the audience's main objection as "zkoušel/a jsem už
spoustu kurzů a nefungovalo to" — this pass gives that line its first actual
quoted backing (three testimonials pointing the same way), still **medium**
per the prompted-format cap, not the higher confidence the plain sentence in
business.md implied.

## 6. The transformation they describe wanting, in their language

- **A daily habit that sticks, not a one-off effort:** "opravdu jsem si
  vytvořila denní návyk, už si ani neumím představit den bez angličtiny"
  (Daniela Tájková). Echoed by Marie D.: "Každý den si prostě musím projet
  kousek." **medium.**
- **Visible forward progress, not just refreshing old knowledge:** "Konečně
  mám pocit, že se posouvám někam dál než k oprašování toho, co už jsem kdysi
  uměla" (Daniela Tájková). **low** (one source, though it is a sharp,
  specific phrase worth keeping verbatim for copy).

## 7. What they believe success hinges on

- A structured plan that does not demand perfection: "Vaše systematické a
  příjemně nesmlouvavé vedení... Samozřejmě jsem nestihl udělat všechny úkoly,
  ale mám dokonalou představu, jak s materiály pracovat" (Vít Bělík). **low**
  but specific — success credited to the plan's forgiving structure, not to
  the student's own discipline.

## 8. Objections seen in the wild

- Age as a disqualifier ("moc jsem nedoufala" at 48). **medium**, per section 3.
- Skepticism that this course is different from ones already tried
  ("jak moc se kurz liší od toho, co všichni známe" — phrased as a relieved
  surprise after the fact, which means the objection existed going in).
  **low.**

## 9. Interpretation — what I read between the lines

This is interpretation, not a finding, and it rests only on the quotes above:
the testimonials that carry the most emotional weight are not about the
content (grammar, vocabulary) at all — they are about the *feeling* of being
capable, given permission to progress imperfectly, and noticing habit
forming. If this reads true, it suggests LCEnglish's audience responds less
to "here is what you will learn" and more to "here is proof you are not too
old / too far behind / too inconsistent to make this stick" — but this is my
reading of six old quotes, not a confirmed segment. It would need fresh,
unprompted evidence (a support email, a comment, a survey answer given without
being asked "how did it help you") before it should carry real weight in a
new plan.
