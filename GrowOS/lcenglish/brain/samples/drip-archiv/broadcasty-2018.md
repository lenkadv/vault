## 2018-05-09 — The stories we don't know

- Interní název: The stories we don't know
- Odesílatel: Lenka & Abbey | Lights Camera English!
- Publikum: All people

Two years ago, I joined my kids in playing an augmented-reality mobile game called Ingress. It involved running around the city and fighting for "portals". The fighting was virtual, of course, I am a pacifist :-)

The "portals" were real, though - various places of cultural significance, such as public art, landmarks, or monuments. It was then that I realized how many little memorials commemorating people killed during the Prague Uprising there were near where we live.

Karel Zdvihal. Josef Briscejn. Marta Vichrová... Who were they? What was their life like? What was their story?

Ever since, the 8th of May is not just a day off for me. I may never get to know their stories, but I feel connected. They were people just like us, living then where we live now, and we shouldn't forget.

So I just wanted to tell you that. And I told Abbey, too :-)

I'm not much of a planner.

When I travel, I prefer letting things happen. I don't need to see major sights where all the tourists flock. What I like doing instead is getting lost. Believe me, getting lost when you travel is a blessing. Because while being lost, you find places you would never see otherwise. A charming local market. Kids playing in a courtyard. A bench with a spectacular view that no guide knows about.

There is probably only one better way of discovering a foreign country than getting lost, and that's experiencing it with locals. With people who avoid touristy stuff not only because they are not tourists, but because they know better. They know what to see, where to go and what to do.

So when I travel and I have a chance to do things with locals, I don't think twice. Which is exactly why I'm going to follow Abbey's lead and do every single thing she suggests for our trip to England. (As long as we stop for a cup of coffee every once in a while.)

Když přijde na cestování, nejsem žádný velký plánovač.

Raději nechávám věci jen tak plynout. Nepotřebuju nutně vidět ty největší turistické atrakce, kam se hrnou davy lidí. Mnohem raději v cizině bloudím. Věřte mi, jednou za čas si pořádně zabloudit je pravé požehnání.

Protože jen při bloudění najdete místa, na která vás žádný průvodce nezavede. Maličkou tržnici, kam chodí nakupovat jenom babky z okolí. Děti hrající fotbal na dvoře kláštera. Lavičku s ohromujícím výhledem na vršku kopce, kam se jinak nikomu nechce šplhat.

Kromě bloudění je pak už jen jedna další cesta, jak poznat cizinu zevnitř, a to je zažít ji s místními. S lidmi, kteří se vyhýbají "turistickému" nejen proto, že nejsou turisté, ale protože vědí, kam zamířit za ještě lepšími zážitky. Vědí, na co se podívat, vědí, kam jít, vědí, co dělat.

Takže pokud mám šanci zažít něco s místními, ani chvilku neváhám. A přesně proto nechám radostně na Abbey, aby vymyslela, co budeme během našeho výletu do Anglie dělat (aspoň tedy pokud bude v plánu sem tam nějaká ta kavárna).
