I love my free time! I guess we all do. But if you asked me: what do you like to do in your free time?… I wouldn’t know what to say.

It’s not that I don’t do anything when I’m not working, but that I do so many different things-I just don’t know how to answer.

I do yoga. I attend dance classes. I like to cook. I’m learning Japanese. I enjoy catching up with my friends over a latte. I sometimes go for walks. I dabble in photography. And I read. A lot.

I don’t often go to art galleries or museums and I’m not a fan of shopping, but I am part of a film club and I love going to the theatre, especially when Abbey is performing. I also love just wandering around the city observing the different types of architecture. I love history.

I have a lot of different interests and hobbies. It’s hard to find time to do them all!

What do you like to do in your free time?
