Lights Camera English is one year old today!

Time flies when you’re having fun. And this year has flown. It seems like only yesterday that Lenka and I were sitting in her kitchen (no surprise there) dreaming of how we could blend her excellent linguistic and teaching skills, with my experience in acting and film.

One cheesecake and fourteen cups of tea later, we were still there. Dreaming up all the ways we could provide fun and light-hearted videos that would support our viewers in learning natural English.

Fifty-two videos (and countless cakes, brunches, crisps, and cups of tea) later, we’re still there. And the ideas haven’t stopped flowing. And we’re so excited about the fun things we’ve got to share with you in year two.

So, there are some changes coming. But one thing remains the same, and that is our goal: to teach natural English the natural way.

So stick around for the journey. There is much fun (and much English) to be had!
