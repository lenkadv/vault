# The five intent types

Both modes of `social-strategy` read this file: Strategy mode uses it to size
the intent mix in the `## Social` section of `brain/plan.md`; Weekly plan mode
uses it to pick each slot's intent and shape its hook. `social-write`, once it
exists, reads the same five names off the weekly-plan item, so the list stays
fixed at exactly these five and nothing gets renamed casually.

For hook wording, see `system/creative-library/hooks.md`. For platform format
and length, see the matching file in `system/creative-library/platforms/`. This
file only covers what each intent is for and when to reach for it.

---

## 1. Teach

**Purpose:** build authority, earn saves and shares, give a follower a reason
to keep following.
**What it is:** a real how-to, a tip, a named step from `brain/methodology.md`,
a myth corrected. Teaching THIS business's method beats generic advice, so
ground it in `brain/methodology.md` or `brain/business.md` when either has
something to say.
**Hooks that fit:** the number hook, the mistake hook, the problem named
plainly (see `hooks.md`).

## 2. Story

**Purpose:** build connection and trust. The most human intent, and the one
that keeps a feed from reading like a brochure.
**What it is:** a real, dated moment from `brain/stories/`, a behind-the-scenes
detail, a lesson learned the hard way. Never an invented anecdote — if
`brain/stories/` is thin, say so rather than inventing a moment.
**Hooks that fit:** the story open, the before-and-after (see `hooks.md`).

## 3. Proof

**Purpose:** show it works, without bragging. Establish credibility through a
real result, not an adjective.
**What it is:** a testimonial or number from `brain/proof/`, used exactly as
written, with a plain source. A business with an empty `proof/` folder cannot
carry a real proof post yet — say so and lean the mix toward teach or story
until proof exists, rather than writing a proof post with nothing behind it.
**Hooks that fit:** the before-and-after, the curiosity gap (see `hooks.md`).
**Hard rule:** never lead with a dollar figure alone. Frame around the
transformation the number represents, not the number as a flex.

## 4. Promo

**Purpose:** convert a follower who already trusts the business into a lead
or a customer. The one intent that asks for something back.
**What it is:** a direct mention of the offer from `brain/business.md`, a
lead magnet, a clear call to act. Earn it: this is the intent to keep
smallest in the mix, and it reads worst when it shows up too often relative
to the other four.
**Hooks that fit:** the bold claim, the callout (see `hooks.md`).
**Frequency:** the lightest slice of the mix. A useful starting ratio is
roughly one promo post for every ten to fifteen posts of the other four
combined; adjust down further for a business with a thin `proof/` folder,
since an ask lands worse with nothing to back it.

## 5. Engage

**Purpose:** boost replies and comments, make the feed feel like a
conversation rather than a broadcast.
**What it is:** a real question drawn from an objection in `brain/audience.md`
or a genuinely open question about the business's own work. Low friction, no
lesson attached, no CTA riding along.
**Hooks that fit:** the question, the callout (see `hooks.md`).

---

## A starting weekly ratio

Not a rule, a starting point to adjust once the business's own `brain/proof/`
and `brain/stories/` are read. In the absence of anything that argues
otherwise:

| Intent | Starting share |
|---|---|
| Teach | ~35-40% |
| Story | ~20% |
| Engage | ~15-20% |
| Proof | ~10-15% |
| Promo | ~5-10% |

Two adjustments that override this default every time:

- **Thin `proof/`.** Drop the proof share toward zero and move it to teach or
  story until real results exist to draw on. Never fill a proof slot with an
  unsourced claim to hit the ratio.
- **Thin `stories/`.** Same move, in the other direction: drop story toward
  teach until real, dated stories exist.

The ratio is a shape to reach for, never a quota that forces an invented post
into an empty slot.
