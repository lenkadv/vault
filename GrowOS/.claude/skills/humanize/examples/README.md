# Examples

Three before/after pairs showing the big rules in action. Every business, name,
number, and quote in these files is constructed for demonstration - nothing here
comes from a real customer, and nothing here may be pasted into real work. When
polishing for a real business, examples like these get regenerated from that
business's own brain.

- `slop-rescue.md` - a tell-heavy draft taken through the full method.
- `clean-but-dead.md` - copy with zero tells that still fails, and why.
- `leave-it-alone.md` - the CLEAN short-circuit: good human writing, untouched.
