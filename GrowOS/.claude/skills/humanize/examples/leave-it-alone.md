# Example: leave it alone

A constructed owner-written update for an invented bike repair shop. This is what
the CLEAN short-circuit is for: the only correct edit is none.

## The input

> We closed for three days in June because Marta broke her wrist, and about
> fifteen of you still left kind notes on the door. So: she is fine, the cast
> comes off next month, and she has opinions about one-handed brake bleeds now.
> Repairs are running two days slower than usual while she heals. If your bike
> is stuck with us right now, thanks for the patience. The coffee is on us when
> you pick up.

## The correct response

Score it (it comes back low, no hard tells, varied rhythm, real specifics, real
voice). Then say, in one line:

> This is shippable as it is - I did not change anything.

That is the whole job. No tightening "anyway", no swapped synonyms, no invented
improvements. "PASS" means unchanged is shippable; gentleness on already-human
writing is part of the standard, not a courtesy.
