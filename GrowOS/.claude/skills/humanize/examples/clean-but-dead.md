# Example: clean but dead

A constructed social post for an invented bookkeeping service. It would score
near zero on the scorer - no tells at all - and it still fails review. This is
the case the personality stage exists for.

## Before (score: ~0, verdict: fix - dead test)

> Good bookkeeping helps your business. When your records are current, you can
> make better decisions. Many small businesses fall behind on their books. We
> help owners stay on top of their numbers. Get in touch to learn more.

Why it fails the dead test (2+ conditions true):

- (a) No opinion or stance anywhere.
- (b) No concrete specific - not one number, name, or date.
- (d) Swap in any competitor's name and every sentence stays equally true.

## Conservative pass (still held; nothing invented)

> Good bookkeeping helps you make better decisions. Many small businesses fall
> behind on their books.
>
> We help owners keep their records current and stay on top of their numbers.
> Get in touch to learn more.

What changed: the rhythm is less flat, but the source is too thin to restore a
real owner stance or concrete detail safely. Hold it and ask the owner for source
material. The brain cannot donate a nicer scene merely because the draft is dead.
