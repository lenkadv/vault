# Example: slop rescue

A constructed newsletter draft for an invented dog-grooming shop. Voice snapshot
(what its voice.md says): warm, blunt, a little dry; short paragraphs; says
"wriggler" for nervous dogs; never uses hype words.

## Before (score: high, many hard tells)

> Whether you're a busy professional or a devoted pet parent, our comprehensive
> grooming services showcase a seamless experience for your furry friend.
> Additionally, our meticulous team leverages cutting-edge techniques, ensuring
> comfort for every dog. It's worth noting that regular grooming is important.
> This isn't just grooming - it's a transformation - a whole new dog!
>
> In conclusion, book today and delve into the difference!

## Findings (evidence-cited, the top ones)

- [hard] E05 vocab cluster: "comprehensive", "showcase", "seamless",
  "meticulous", "leverages", "delve" - six list words in 70 words.
- [hard] E15 + E33 opener: "Whether you're a busy professional..." - any business
  could say this line.
- [hard] E10: "Additionally," opener. E09: "It's worth noting". E11: "In
  conclusion,". E12: three dashes in one paragraph. E07: "This isn't just X -
  it's Y". E17: ", ensuring comfort...".
- [judgment] E16: "regular grooming is important" - no one alive disagrees.
- [judgment] E32/E36: zero specifics, zero real moments.

## After (tells gone, facts unchanged, no bonus scene)

> Busy professional or devoted pet parent, your dog gets a full grooming service
> designed to feel straightforward from start to finish.
>
> Our careful team uses up-to-date grooming techniques to keep every dog
> comfortable. Regular grooming matters. A good groom can make a dog look
> completely different.
>
> Book today and see the difference.

What changed, in one line: removed the vocab cluster and stock frames, varied the
rhythm, and kept the source claims without importing a shop scene from the brain.

This is **not shippable** yet. The mechanical slop is gone, but the source is too
thin to supply a real stance, specific, or lived moment, and "Regular grooming
matters" remains generic because its meaning cannot simply be deleted.
