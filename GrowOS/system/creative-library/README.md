# The GrowOS library

This folder is a small starter kit of marketing patterns that every business in
this install can draw from. Think of it as a shelf of proven shapes: ways to open
a post so people keep reading, and ways to lay a post out.

Two things to know:

- **We ship and maintain this folder.** When you update GrowOS, the files in here
  get replaced with the newest versions. So do not save your own notes here.
- **What your business learns lives in that business**, in its own `library/`
  folder. It is yours forever and updates never touch it. Keeping it there rather
  than somewhere central is deliberate: if you run more than one business, a
  pattern proven for one must never turn up in another one's drafts.

## What is here

- **hooks.md**: a catalog of hook patterns. A hook is the first line of a post,
  the part that decides whether someone reads the rest. Each entry says what the
  pattern is, when it fits, and shows a plain example.
- **post-styles.md**: a catalog of post shapes. Each entry is a way to structure
  a whole post, with a note on when it works best and a plain example.
- **email-types.md**: a catalog of eight email types (newsletter, promo, welcome,
  nurture, launch, win-back, cart-abandon, trial-nurture). Each entry says what it
  is, when to use it, how it's built, and shows a plain example outline.
- **platforms/**: one short craft guide per platform — `linkedin.md`,
  `instagram.md`, `facebook.md`, `x.md`, `tiktok.md`, `threads.md`. Each says what
  works there, the format that fits, which hooks from `hooks.md` suit it best, and
  hashtag/emoji defaults for that platform. A business's own `voice.md` or
  `brand.md` always wins over these defaults.
- **ads/**: a static ad-visual library for Meta ad creative, used by the
  `ads-meta-create` skill. `ads/templates/` is a set of HTML cards rendered to
  PNG with no AI (see `ads/templates/CATALOG.md` and `ads/RENDER.md`);
  `ads/styles/` is a catalog of AI-image prompt recipes (`ads/styles/CATALOG.md`);
  `ads/carousels/CONCEPTS.md` holds multi-slide swipe concepts; `ads/PLAYBOOK.md`
  is the shared method for choosing a format and adapting it to your business.

These are starting points, not rules. The best hook for your business is the one
that sounds like you and speaks to your audience. Use these to get unstuck, then
make them yours.
