# X

X rewards a sharp point made fast, in public, where the reply counts more
than the like. The crowd here moves quickly: builders, media, marketers, and
anyone following a live conversation. A post that starts a real exchange in
the replies travels further than one that just gets liked and scrolled past.

## Format

- **280 characters is a hard limit** per post. If the idea needs more room,
  write it as a thread: the first post is the hook, each one after delivers
  a single beat.
- **Say the whole thing in the first line.** There is no fold to hook past —
  the whole post is usually visible at once, so don't waste words getting
  there.
- **Keep links out of the main post.** A link kills reach. If you need one,
  put it in a reply instead.
- **Use line breaks inside a single post** to give a punchy line room to
  land, instead of cramming everything into one paragraph.
- **Rich media helps.** An image, short video, or poll outperforms plain
  text.
- **Replies are worth more than likes here.** A post that invites a real
  answer, not just agreement, tends to travel further.

## Hooks that fit

See `../hooks.md`. The bold claim, the contrarian take, and the number hook
all suit X's pace — short, sharp, and built to make someone stop and react.
The question sequence works well when you're opening a thread.

## Hashtags and emoji

As a starting point: hashtags are sparse here — 0, or 1 only if it's
genuinely relevant (an event or a live conversation people are searching).
Emoji used lightly, mostly for tone or emphasis, not decoration. If the
business's own `brain/voice.md` or `brand.md` says otherwise, that wins.

## What to avoid

- Burying the actual point past the first line.
- Turning a single idea into a thread it didn't need to be.
- A link in the main post — it will quietly kill your reach.
- Over-hashtagging. It reads like a decade ago.
