# TikTok

TikTok rewards a video that earns its next second, every second. The crowd
here came for entertainment first, and the algorithm shows your video to
strangers based on how it performs, not how many followers you have. A great
idea with a slow start never gets seen — the hook has to land before the
caption does any work at all.

## Format

- **The hook lives in the video, in the first one to three seconds** —
  spoken, shown, or both. If it doesn't grab attention immediately, the
  scroll wins before the caption is even read.
- **On-screen text should echo the spoken hook**, not just repeat the
  caption. It's a safety net for anyone watching on mute.
- **The caption is secondary.** Keep it short — a bit of context or a
  question. It supports the video; it doesn't carry the post.
- **Native trends, sounds, and effects boost discovery.** A trending sound
  with your own angle on it travels further than an original one with no
  cultural hook.
- **Authenticity beats polish.** A rough, real video regularly outperforms a
  slick, ad-like one. Save the high production for when it actually earns
  its keep.
- **Length:** most content lands best in the 15 to 60 second range. Longer
  works for a real tutorial or story if people are actually staying to
  watch.

## Hooks that fit

See `../hooks.md`, but remember the hook has to work as something SAID or
SHOWN, not just written. The mistake hook and the number hook both translate
well to video — "3 things I got wrong" or "the mistake everyone makes with
X," said straight to camera in the first second.

## Hashtags and emoji

As a starting point: 3 to 5 hashtags, mixing one or two broad discovery tags
with a couple that match your specific niche. Emoji are fine in the caption,
matching the casual tone of the video. If the business's own
`brain/voice.md` or `brand.md` says otherwise, follow that instead.

## What to avoid

- A slow intro before the hook. You have one to three seconds, not ten.
- A caption that just repeats what's already on screen.
- Video that looks and sounds like a polished ad — it reads as an ad and
  gets skipped.
- Chasing every trend regardless of fit. A trend only works if it fits the
  message.
