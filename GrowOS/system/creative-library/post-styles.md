# Post styles

A hook opens a post. A style shapes the whole thing. Below are six timeless post
shapes that work on almost any channel. Each says when it fits and shows a plain
example outline you can fill with your own words.

None of these are rules. They are shapes to lean on when you are staring at a blank
page. Mix them, break them, and always keep the voice yours.

---

## 1. The tip list
**When to use:** you know something useful and want to give it away fast.
**Shape:** a hook, then a short numbered list, then one line that ties it together.
**Example:**
> Three ways to [get result] without [common cost].
> 1. [tip one, one sentence]
> 2. [tip two, one sentence]
> 3. [tip three, one sentence]
> Start with number two. It is the one people skip.

## 2. The story
**When to use:** you have a real moment that carries a lesson.
**Shape:** set the scene, share the turn, land the point. Keep it short and true.
**Example:**
> [Set the scene: where you were, what was at stake.]
> [The turn: what happened, what you learned.]
> [The point: what it means for the reader.]

## 3. The how-to
**When to use:** you want to teach one clear thing, step by step.
**Shape:** name the outcome, list the steps, add the one tip that saves them pain.
**Example:**
> Here is how to [do the thing] in [time or steps].
> First, [step]. Then, [step]. Finally, [step].
> The trick most people miss: [the tip].

## 4. The honest opinion
**When to use:** you have a real view and are willing to stand behind it.
**Shape:** state the take, give your reason, invite the pushback.
**Example:**
> I think [belief], and I know not everyone will agree.
> Here is why: [reason, plainly].
> Tell me where I am wrong.

## 5. The question
**When to use:** you want to start a conversation, not give a lecture.
**Shape:** one short setup, one clear question, then get out of the way.
**Example:**
> [One line of context that frames the question.]
> [The question, asked simply.]

## 6. The plain announcement
**When to use:** you have news, a launch, or an offer to share.
**Shape:** say what it is, say who it helps, say what to do next. No hype.
**Example:**
> [What is new, in one sentence.]
> [Who it is for and the one problem it solves.]
> [The single next step, clearly.]

---

**One rule for all six:** one post, one idea. If you are trying to say three
things, you have three posts. The clearest post almost always wins.
