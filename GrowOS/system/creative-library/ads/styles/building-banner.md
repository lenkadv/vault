# Building Confession Banner

**Description:** A hand-painted confession banner hanging from a small business building at dusk.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal dusk photo of a small two-story business building (a modest studio or shop), streetlights just on. A giant hand-painted white banner hangs from the upper windows, rippling slightly, with huge handwritten letters that read exactly: '[BANNER HEADLINE]' Below in smaller handwriting exactly: '[BANNER SUBLINE]' A single warm office window still lit. Cinematic but believable, shot on iPhone at dusk. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** BANNER HEADLINE = "I STOPPED DOING THIS MYSELF."; BANNER SUBLINE = "(the business is fine. better, actually.)"

## Why this works

A confession blown up to architectural scale on a real banner is an absurd staging choice, which is exactly what makes it stop a scroll, while keeping the actual words honest and claim-free keeps it believable rather than gimmicky.

## Audience fit

Solo operators and small business owners ready to admit they let go of a task, works as a solution-aware, slightly self-aware moment.

## Adapting to your business

Write the confession as your audience's real "I stopped doing X myself" line. Keep the words free of specific metrics or timeframes; the banner's power is the honest confession, not a manufactured result.
