# Animated Feature Poster

**Description:** Your founder rendered as a big-studio 3D animated character in a movie-poster layout: soft volumetric lighting, smooth subsurface skin shading, expressive eyes, identity preserved.

**Prompt template:**
```
Big-studio 3D CGI animated-movie poster. The hero character is [YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing] rendered as a 3D animated character, identity recognizably preserved (same face shape, same hair, same expression language). Soft volumetric lighting, smooth subsurface skin shading, expressive eyes, slight stylization but clearly recognizable as them. Title block at top in a bold animated-movie typeface: "[FILM TITLE OR HEADLINE]". Tagline below in smaller text: "[SUB-HEADLINE]". Background: [a cinematic scene in the founder's world, modern home office at golden hour / abstract digital landscape / animated city skyline / cozy desk with a glowing screen]. [IDENTITY-LOCK LINE, one sentence telling the model to keep your founder's exact facial structure, bone structure, nose shape, jawline, and skin texture identical across every generation, so they read as the same real person in every ad]. Cinema-grade composition. Vertical or square framing. No watermarks. Single character only.
```

**Variations:** Background scene, expression, tagline text, color palette of the scene.

## Why this works

Turning a real founder into a big, friendly animated character borrows the warmth and universal likeability of feature-animation without needing a studio budget, and identity preservation keeps it recognizably them rather than a generic mascot.

## Audience fit

Buyers who respond to a fun, feel-good, larger-than-life brand moment, strongest for a launch or a big announcement rather than a serious authority pitch.

## Adapting to your business

Write the title block as your actual headline and pick a background scene from your founder's real world. The Steady Coach could run this with a title like "The Comeback Season" over a cozy home-studio scene. Keep the identity-lock instruction strict; a poster that only loosely resembles the founder undercuts the whole point of using their real face.
