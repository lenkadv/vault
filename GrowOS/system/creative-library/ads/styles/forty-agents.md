# One Human, Forty Workers

**Description:** One calm person at a desk in a dark room, surrounded by a constellation of dozens of glowing holographic workers, a desk plaque naming the team.

**Prompt template:**
```
Proudly artificial stylized 3D scene: one calm person at a desk in the center of a dark room, surrounded by a constellation of forty tiny glowing holographic worker figures, each busy at a floating panel (writing, charts, envelopes, megaphones). A small desk plaque reads exactly: '[DESK PLAQUE]'. Cinematic rim light, clearly artificial, awe not chaos.
```

**Example fill:** DESK PLAQUE = "TEAM: 1 HUMAN"

## Why this works

Rendering an abstract claim, "a whole team, run by one person," as a literal, obviously-artificial scene earns attention without pretending to be real, and staging it as awe rather than chaos matches the aspiration the buyer actually holds: commanding leverage, not drowning in it.

## Audience fit

Solo operators who already believe in using tools and automation to punch above their size, and want to picture themselves at the center of that, in command, not replaced by it.

## Adapting to your business

Rewrite the plaque and re-task the glowing workers to your business's real deliverables. Willow & Wren could re-task them to packing, photographing, and answering messages. Keep the single calm human at the center and the obviously artificial swarm; making it look too real tips the mood from awe into unsettling.
