# Phone Screen Results

**Description:** Your founder (or just a hand) holding a phone toward the camera to show a real result on screen.

**Mode:** Edit (founder's face visible) or generate (hands-only close-up, no reference photos needed). Use the matching template below.

**Prompt template (with face, edit mode):**
```
[YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing], holding their phone toward the camera to show the screen. The phone screen shows [a notification / a real results dashboard / a real results graph] (slightly blurred but colorful, and true to your actual data, never invented). They have an [excited expression, eyebrows raised / knowing smirk]. Wearing a casual [dark t-shirt / gray henley]. Standing in a [home office / kitchen] with a clean background. Bright natural light. Photorealistic, iPhone photo taken by a friend. No watermarks. Single person only. Correct hand anatomy, exactly five fingers per hand.
```

**Prompt template (hands only, generate mode, no founder photo needed):**
```
Close-up of a hand holding a smartphone. The phone screen shows [a real upward-trending graph / a real payment notification / a real email open-rate stat] (true to your actual data, never invented). The hand has natural skin texture. Background is a blurred [wooden desk with a laptop / office setting]. Warm natural lighting. Focus is crisp on the phone screen. Photorealistic, macro photography. No watermarks. Correct hand anatomy, exactly five fingers.
```

**Variations:** With or without the founder's face, and what the screen shows (notification, dashboard, email).

## Why this works

A phone held up to the camera reads as a caught, unstaged moment rather than a designed graphic, so a real number lands as proof instead of a marketing claim. The hands-only version keeps the same effect without needing anyone's face.

## Audience fit

Any buyer who responds better to a real result than to a promise, especially useful once you have a genuine milestone worth showing.

## Adapting to your business

Only ever show a real screen from your own data; never invent a number or metric to display, the whole point of this style is that it looks caught, not staged, and a faked result breaks trust the moment someone notices. Willow & Wren could show a real order-notification count; Summit Books & Tax could show a real client's completed filing confirmation (with permission).
