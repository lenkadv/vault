# Dollhouse Cutaway

**Description:** An isometric dollhouse cutaway: a family at dinner upstairs, a small robot team working in the basement.

**Prompt template:**
```
Isometric dollhouse cutaway render, stylized 3D, proudly artificial: a two-story house sliced open. Upstairs: a warmly lit dining room where a family eats dinner together. Downstairs basement: a tiny glowing office where five small friendly robots work at desks under a banner that reads exactly: '[BANNER WORD, e.g. "ORDERS," "BOOKKEEPING," "ALL OF IT"]'. Cozy miniature detail, warm-cool light contrast, one clear story in one glance. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** BANNER WORD = "THE BUSY WORK".

## Why this works

Showing two truths at once, a free life upstairs and steady work happening below, tells a complete story in a single glance without needing a caption to explain it.

## Audience fit

Owners who want their business to keep running without eating their family time, works well for a freedom or work-life balance angle.

## Adapting to your business

Change the banner word to what actually runs in your background. Keep the cutaway structure and the warm-cool light contrast between the two floors; collapsing them into one room loses the whole point of the metaphor.
