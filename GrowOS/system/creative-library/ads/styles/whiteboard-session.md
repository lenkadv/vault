# Founder Whiteboard Session

**Description:** Your founder at a whiteboard mid-explanation, writing out a framework or diagram.

**Prompt template:**
```
[YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing], standing at a [large whiteboard / glass whiteboard] in a modern home office. They are mid-writing with a [blue/black] marker, drawing a [simple funnel / framework diagram / bullet points]. They wear a casual [dark gray henley / black t-shirt]. The whiteboard has some existing notes in different colors. Natural daylight from a window. [Shot angle]. Photorealistic, [camera style]. No watermarks. Single person only.
```

**Variations:** Shot angle (below, side, close-up on hand and board), marker color, what's on the board.

## Why this works

A founder mid-explanation at a whiteboard reads as teaching, not selling, which lowers the audience's guard and positions the founder as the expert in the room, even a home-office room.

## Audience fit

Buyers who want to understand the mechanism before they buy, works well for framework reveals, "how this actually works" posts, and authority-building content.

## Adapting to your business

Write the actual diagram or framework on the board, don't leave it generic. Summit Books & Tax could sketch a simple three-step filing process; The Steady Coach could sketch their signature program outline. Keep the founder mid-motion with the marker still in hand; a finished, static board loses the "caught mid-thought" energy.
