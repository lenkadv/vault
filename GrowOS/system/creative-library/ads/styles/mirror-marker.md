# Bathroom Mirror Line

**Description:** A single line written by hand on a bathroom mirror in dry-erase marker.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal photo of a bathroom mirror in morning light, toothbrush cup visible at bottom. Written on the glass in black dry-erase marker, natural handwriting, reads exactly: '[MIRROR LINE]'. Slight marker smudges, real reflections, shot on iPhone, candid. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** MIRROR LINE = "You didn't start a business to do this part."

## Why this works

A handwritten line on a genuinely personal surface, a bathroom mirror, feels private rather than performed, so it lands as a quiet truth the viewer recognizes in themselves instead of a pitch aimed at them.

## Audience fit

Buyers who are tired and would respond to being seen before being sold to, best used early in a funnel to build recognition, not as a closing offer.

## Adapting to your business

Write the line as the exact unspoken thought your buyer has about the part of the job they didn't sign up for. The Steady Coach could write "You didn't start coaching to chase invoices." Keep the handwriting rough and the mirror candid; a clean, designed version loses the intimacy this style is built on.
