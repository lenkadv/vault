# Approve the Week

**Description:** An over-the-shoulder close-up of a thumb hovering over one big "approve" button, the whole week's work waiting on a single yes.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal close-up photo over someone's shoulder of a thumb hovering above a phone screen that shows one large clean button reading exactly: '[BUTTON LABEL]' with a small line above it exactly: '[SUBLINE]'. Morning kitchen bokeh, large legible UI, shot on iPhone.
```

**Example fill:** BUTTON LABEL = "Approve week"; SUBLINE = "5 ready".

## Why this works

Mirroring the real interaction of a review-and-approve workflow, rather than illustrating it abstractly, makes the mechanism legible in one glance: you didn't have to make it, you just have to say yes.

## Audience fit

Buyers who want to stop creating and start approving, works best once the audience already understands roughly what the product does.

## Adapting to your business

Change the button label and subline to your product's real approve-moment: "Send batch," "Publish all," "Ship 4 orders." Keep the over-the-shoulder framing and the single, oversized button; a screen crowded with multiple buttons loses the "one yes handles it all" read.
