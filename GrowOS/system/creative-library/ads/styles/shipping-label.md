# Shipping Label Gag

**Description:** A cardboard shipping box on a doorstep whose printed label carries the joke.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal close-up photo of a cardboard shipping box on a doorstep, morning light. A large white shipping label on the box reads exactly: '[LABEL HEADLINE]' and below in smaller print exactly: '[LABEL SUBLINE]'. Realistic label typography, barcode, slightly curled corner, shot on iPhone, shallow depth of field. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** LABEL HEADLINE = "CONTENTS: 1 FULL-TIME TEAM"; LABEL SUBLINE = "HANDLE WITH CARE. WORKS NIGHTS."

## Why this works

A delivered box gives a purely digital service physical weight, it reads as "this arrived" rather than "you signed up," and the deadpan label print is where the actual joke lives.

## Audience fit

Buyers of digital services or software who respond to a light, tangible "it's here" moment rather than a straight feature pitch.

## Adapting to your business

Rewrite both label lines for what your business actually delivers. Willow & Wren, whose product genuinely ships in a box, could lean into this literally with "CONTENTS: EXACTLY WHAT YOU ORDERED." Keep the realistic label typography and barcode; a cartoonish label undercuts the deadpan realism.
