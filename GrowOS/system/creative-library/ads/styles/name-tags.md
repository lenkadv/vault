# Six Name Tags

**Description:** A fictional owner wearing six stacked "HELLO I'M" role stickers down their apron.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal deadpan portrait of a fictional business owner standing in their shop wearing SIX stacked 'HELLO I'M' name stickers down their apron, each handwritten exactly: '[ROLE 1]', '[ROLE 2]', '[ROLE 3]', '[ROLE 4]', '[ROLE 5]', '[ROLE 6]'. Tired but wry expression, flat even light, portrait style, painfully relatable. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** ROLES = "the owner", "the ads person", "the copywriter", "the designer", "support", "the intern".

**Note:** this uses a fictional subject, so it stays generate mode. Do not pass your founder's reference photos into this one.

## Why this works

Stacking every role onto one body, on one apron, makes "team of one" literal in a single deadpan portrait, and the wry rather than exhausted expression keeps it funny instead of grim.

## Audience fit

Solo operators and very small teams who genuinely fill every one of those roles themselves.

## Adapting to your business

Rewrite the six roles to your own real job list. Summit Books & Tax could use "the accountant," "the receptionist," "the IT department," and so on. Keep the flat, even light and the deadpan expression; an obviously posed, cheerful expression undercuts the joke.
