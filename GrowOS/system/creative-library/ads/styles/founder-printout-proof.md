# Founder Printout Proof

**Description:** Your founder holding a thick stack of freshly printed pages proudly, like a newspaper.

**Prompt template:**
```
[YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing]. Photo of the person from the reference photos at a standing desk, holding a thick stack of freshly printed pages proudly like a newspaper, slight tired grin. The top page header reads exactly: '[PAGE HEADER]'. Morning home-office light, bookshelf behind, photoreal, shot on iPhone. No watermarks. Photorealistic quality. Single person only. Correct hand anatomy, exactly five fingers per hand.
```

**Example fill:** PAGE HEADER = "THIS WEEK'S OUTPUT".

**Founder photo (edit mode):** pass 4 to 6 evenly-lit reference photos of your founder as image input, mixing front, three-quarter, and profile angles, and prepend the identity-lock line above. See this pack's intro for how to build your own reference set.

## Why this works

Holding the actual output in your hands is a physical proof-of-work: it says "this genuinely got made this week" without a single word of copy doing the convincing.

## Audience fit

Buyers who want proof of consistent output over promises of it, works well for a weekly or recurring cadence.

## Adapting to your business

Change the page header to whatever your founder actually produced that week. Summit Books & Tax could print "THIS MONTH'S FILINGS"; The Steady Coach could print "THIS WEEK'S LESSONS". Keep the tired-but-proud grin; a stiff, posed smile undercuts the honest, caught-in-the-act feel.
