# Calendar Grind and Relief

**Description:** A wall calendar where every single day is scrawled with the same tired word, most crossed out in red, with a fresh sticky note signaling the end of that grind.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal photo of a wall calendar for one month where every single day has '[CALENDAR SCRAWL]' scrawled in tired handwriting, most days crossed out in red. Stuck over the last week, one clean yellow sticky reads exactly: '[STICKY LINE]'. Kitchen light, candid angle.
```

**Example fill:** CALENDAR SCRAWL = "POST"; STICKY LINE = "never again. it's handled."

## Why this works

Seeing the exact same tired word repeated across every square of a real calendar dramatizes a grind far better than a sentence could, and the one fresh sticky note at the end reads as genuine relief rather than a sales pitch.

## Audience fit

Buyers stuck in a repetitive, energy-draining task who would recognize the specific grind you name on the calendar.

## Adapting to your business

Swap the repeated word to your audience's real recurring task ("INVOICE," "FOLLOW UP," "RESTOCK") and write the sticky as the honest relief once that task is handled. Keep the tired handwriting and the red crossouts; a neat calendar loses the lived-in feeling this style depends on.
