# Editorial Identity Poster

**Description:** A magazine-poster composition with your founder as the singular hero, identity preserved exactly, paired with a bold headline against a minimal color block.

**Prompt template:**
```
Editorial poster composition. [YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing] is the only subject, identity preserved exactly. Strong central or slightly off-center placement, [waist-up / portrait crop / full-body]. Background is a solid color block in [YOUR BRAND'S PRIMARY OR NEUTRAL COLOR]. Bold serif typography reading "[HEADLINE]" placed [top-left / bottom-edge / spanning the right margin]. [IDENTITY-LOCK LINE, one sentence telling the model to keep your founder's exact facial structure, bone structure, nose shape, jawline, and skin texture identical across every generation, so they read as the same real person in every ad]. Magazine-grade composition, premium minimalism, slight paper grain. No watermarks. Single person only.
```

**Variations:** Background color, typography placement, crop tightness.

## Why this works

A single hero on a flat color block with bold type borrows the visual grammar of a magazine cover, which reads as authority without saying the word. It works because there is nothing else competing for attention: one face, one line, one color.

## Audience fit

Buyers who want to see the person behind the business before they trust the offer, useful for launch announcements or a founder's first big brand moment.

## Adapting to your business

Swap the headline and the background color to your palette; the composition itself needs no other changes. The Steady Coach could run this with a headline like "Coaching, not content creation." Keep the flat color block and the single subject; a busy background undoes the premium feel this style is built for.
