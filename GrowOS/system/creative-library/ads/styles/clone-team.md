# Multiplied at Five Desks

**Description:** The same fictional person multiplied across five desks in one room, each version doing a different job.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal office photo, one impossible premise: the SAME person (an unremarkable fictional business owner in a plain shirt) appears five times at five desks in one room, each version doing a different job: writing on a laptop, sketching something on paper, filming themselves with a phone, sorting mail, pinning a calendar. Identical face and clothes on all five, believable light and shadows, documentary framing. Clearly the same person multiplied. No watermarks.
```

**Note:** this uses a fictional subject, so it stays generate mode. Do not pass your founder's reference photos into this one; if you want your own founder's face, use a founder-photo style instead.

## Why this works

Making "you are the whole team" literal and photoreal at once, one person doing five jobs at once in one room, is the kind of impossible-but-believable image that stops a scroll through sheer pattern interrupt.

## Audience fit

Solo operators and small owner-operated businesses who genuinely wear every hat.

## Adapting to your business

Swap the five jobs to your audience's real roles: packing orders, answering messages, doing the books, restocking, posting online. Keep the identical face and clothes across all five and the documentary, believable lighting; this is the photoreal-but-impossible end of the spectrum, so it should look real everywhere except the premise itself.
