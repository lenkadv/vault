# Founder Approve From Anywhere

**Description:** Your founder relaxed in an airport lounge, tapping their phone to approve something with a small satisfied nod. The phone screen shows a simple, legible UI moment, proof the business runs without them being chained to a desk.

**Prompt template:**
```
[YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing]. Candid photo of the person from the reference photos in an airport lounge chair by the window, planes visible outside, tapping their phone with a small satisfied nod. Their phone screen shows a simple approval UI with a large button that reads exactly: '[BUTTON TEXT]'. Soft terminal light, travel-day realism, shot on iPhone. No watermarks. Photorealistic quality. Correct hand anatomy, exactly five fingers per hand.
```

**Example fill:** BUTTON TEXT = "Approve all 3" (over a short stack of Draft 1 / Draft 2 / Draft 3 cards).

**Founder photo (edit mode):** pass 4 to 6 evenly-lit reference photos of your founder as image input, mixing front, three-quarter, and profile angles, and prepend the identity-lock line above. See this pack's intro for how to build your own reference set.

**Other settings for the same mechanic:** the same "caught glancing at a phone, business is fine without them" moment also works in a supermarket checkout queue glancing at a one-line notification, or walking a bicycle along a street, laughing at something on the phone. Swap the setting, keep the candid glance and the one legible line on screen.

## Why this works

Showing the founder somewhere other than their desk, still calmly in control via one tap, makes "you don't have to be chained to this" concrete instead of a slogan. The legible on-screen text does the explaining so the copy doesn't have to.

## Audience fit

Buyers who dream of stepping away without the business falling apart, strongest for a freedom or "run it from anywhere" angle rather than a pure feature pitch.

## Adapting to your business

Write the on-screen text as your founder's real approve-style action. Willow & Wren could show "Ship 4 orders" ready to tap; Summit Books & Tax could show "Send 2 filings" waiting for approval. Keep the setting ordinary and candid; an obviously staged phone-hold breaks the "still living their life" read.
