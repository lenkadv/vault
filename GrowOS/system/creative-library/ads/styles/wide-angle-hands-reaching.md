# Wide-Angle Hands Reaching

**Description:** An ultra-wide, low-angle shot of your founder leaning over the camera with hands reaching toward the lens, creating dramatic foreground perspective.

**Prompt template:**
```
Ultra wide-angle low-angle portrait of [YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing] leaning over the camera with both hands reaching toward the lens, creating exaggerated foreground perspective. Hands and arms appear larger than the head. Face fully visible above, identity preserved, [excited / amused / surprised / wide-eyed] expression. Wearing [a casual hoodie / t-shirt]. Background is a [bright outdoor scene / warm interior / clean studio wall in a neutral color]. [IDENTITY-LOCK LINE, one sentence telling the model to keep your founder's exact facial structure, bone structure, nose shape, jawline, and skin texture identical across every generation, so they read as the same real person in every ad]. Slight motion blur on hands, lens flare allowed. No watermarks. Single person only. Correct hand anatomy, exactly five fingers per hand.
```

**Variations:** Outdoor vs indoor, expression intensity, what's behind the lens (clean wall, blurred laptop, brand color).

## Why this works

The exaggerated perspective and direct energy toward the camera physically interrupt a passive scroll, it feels like the founder is reaching through the screen, which is an unusually strong pattern break in a feed of flat, static photos.

## Audience fit

Buyers you need to stop cold before they'll read anything else, best used as a scroll-stopping opener rather than a closing or trust-building image.

## Adapting to your business

Match the expression to the hook you're pairing it with (surprised for a curiosity hook, amused for a lighter one). Keep the exaggerated hand perspective; a normal-scale hand loses the pattern-interrupt effect this style depends on.
