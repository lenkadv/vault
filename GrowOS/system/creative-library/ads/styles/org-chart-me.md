# Whiteboard Org Chart

**Description:** A hand-drawn whiteboard org chart where every box says the same repeated word, with one fresh box being added live in a different color marker.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal photo of a whiteboard with a hand-drawn org chart: five boxes connected by lines, each handwritten exactly '[REPEATED ROLE]', except one freshly drawn box at the side being written in a contrasting marker color that reads exactly: '[NEW BOX]'. A hand with the marker exits frame, marker smudges, candid office light, slightly imperfect framing.
```

**Example fill:** REPEATED ROLE = "me" (all five boxes); NEW BOX = "the system".

## Why this works

Seeing the same word fill every box on an org chart makes "I am the whole company" instantly legible, and drawing the new box live, marker still in hand, turns the resolution into something that just happened rather than something being announced.

## Audience fit

Solo operators and very small teams who genuinely run every function themselves and would recognize the joke immediately.

## Adapting to your business

Keep "me" or swap the repeated word to your audience's actual self-label, and change the new box to whatever the real delegate is: a hire, a tool, a process. Keep the whiteboard, the hand still in frame, and the slightly imperfect framing; a tidy, centered chart photo loses the caught-mid-thought realism.
