# Editorial Oversized Prop

**Description:** Your founder in a playful editorial pose with an oversized prop (giant pen, huge phone, comically large mug) as a co-hero. Bright, fun, energetic.

**Prompt template:**
```
Playful fashion-editorial photograph of [YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing] in a casual [pose, sitting cross-legged / leaning back laughing / standing mid-stride / hugging the prop]. They interact with an oversized [PROP, giant ballpoint pen / huge smartphone / enormous notebook / comically large coffee mug / oversized clipboard]. The prop is unrealistically large but in scale to the shot, anchoring the frame. Clean minimal studio background in [YOUR BRAND'S NEUTRAL COLOR]. [IDENTITY-LOCK LINE, one sentence telling the model to keep your founder's exact facial structure, bone structure, nose shape, jawline, and skin texture identical across every generation, so they read as the same real person in every ad]. Energetic, light, fun. Bright, clean, even light. No watermarks. Single person only.
```

**Variations:** Prop type, pose, and prop color (use your accent color for extra scroll-stop).

## Why this works

Scale humor is an easy, instant read: an oversized everyday object next to a real person is funny without needing a caption, and the founder's genuine reaction sells the mood better than any stock photo could.

## Audience fit

Buyers who respond to personality-forward brands and want proof there is a real, likeable person running the show, not just a logo.

## Adapting to your business

Pick a prop tied to your actual work. Summit Books & Tax could use an oversized calculator or receipt; Willow & Wren could use an oversized price tag or measuring tape. Keep the exaggerated scale and the bright, even studio light; a dim or moody light drains the fun out of this one.
