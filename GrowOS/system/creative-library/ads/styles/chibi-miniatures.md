# Chibi Team Miniatures

**Description:** A photoreal shot of your founder as the main hero, surrounded by 4 to 6 cute 3D chibi mini-versions of themselves, each doing a different micro-task for the business.

**Prompt template:**
```
[YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing] standing or sitting center-frame as the realistic main subject, identity preserved. Surrounded by 4-6 cute 3D-style chibi miniatures of the same person (big head, small body, same identity preserved: same face, same hair). Each chibi performs a different micro-action for the business: typing on a tiny laptop, holding a tiny sign that reads exactly "[MICRO-HEADLINE]", drawing on a mini whiteboard, lifting a tiny calendar page, pointing at a mini dashboard. Chibis sit on the founder's shoulders and palms, and near their feet on a desk in front. Soft, warm 3D-animated lighting. Clean studio background in [YOUR BRAND'S NEUTRAL BACKGROUND COLOR]. [IDENTITY-LOCK LINE, one sentence telling the model to keep your founder's exact facial structure, bone structure, nose shape, jawline, and skin texture identical across every generation, so they read as the same real person in every ad]. Playful, joyful, cohesive. No watermarks.
```

**Variations:** Number of chibis (4 to 8), what each one is doing, and the micro-sign text.

## Why this works

One realistic founder plus a swarm of tiny identical copies makes "I handle all of it myself" a literal, charming picture instead of a claim. Because every chibi shares the same face, it reads as one very busy person, not an inflated team.

## Audience fit

Founder-led businesses that want a warm, non-corporate way to show range, everything they personally cover in a day, in a single frame.

## Adapting to your business

Keep the founder as the one realistic figure and let the miniatures carry the specific tasks. For Willow & Wren, swap the micro-actions to packing a box, photographing a product, and answering a DM. For Summit Books & Tax, swap to filing receipts, running a spreadsheet, and stamping a return. Keep the scale contrast between the real founder and the chibis, and the shared identity across every miniature.
