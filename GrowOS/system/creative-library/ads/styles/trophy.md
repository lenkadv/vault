# Sincere Trophy Shot

**Description:** A gleaming brass desk trophy with an engraved plate, a plain labeled folder leaning against its base, shot as a sincere award photo.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal photo of a large gleaming brass trophy dominating the frame, standing on a tidy home-office desk, warm afternoon light, slightly low hero angle. The engraved plate on the trophy base reads exactly: '[TROPHY PLATE TEXT]'. Leaning against the trophy, a plain manila folder with a small clean label that reads exactly: '[FOLDER LABEL]'. Shallow depth of field, dust motes in the light, sincere award-photo framing, shot on iPhone. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** TROPHY PLATE TEXT = "SMALL BUSINESS OF THE YEAR"; FOLDER LABEL = "[YOUR SYSTEM OR OFFER NAME]".

## Why this works

A physical award object, shot sincerely instead of ironically, reads as real the way a rendered badge never does, and pairing it with a plain, humble folder says "the real win is the system," not a fabricated result.

## Audience fit

Buyers who want to feel proud of their business again, works as a quiet confidence signal rather than a proof-of-results claim.

## Adapting to your business

Write the plate as a genuine claim about your business or category, never a fabricated customer metric. Willow & Wren could engrave "MOST-LOVED SMALL SHOP ON THE BLOCK." Keep the sincere, low hero angle and the humble folder leaning against the trophy; a customer number on the plate that you can't back up will cost you trust the moment someone asks about it.
