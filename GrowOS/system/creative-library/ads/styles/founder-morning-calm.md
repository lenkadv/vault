# Founder Morning Calm

**Description:** Your founder in a quiet, ordinary morning moment at home: leaning on the kitchen counter with a coffee, reading their phone with calm focus. No grind, no hustle, just a normal calm morning.

**Prompt template:**
```
[YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing]. Photo of the person from the reference photos leaning on their kitchen counter at 8am, coffee cup in one hand, reading their phone with calm focus, morning sun across the counter. Photoreal, warm, shot on iPhone. No watermarks. Photorealistic quality. Single person only. Correct hand anatomy, exactly five fingers per hand.
```

**Founder photo (edit mode):** pass 4 to 6 evenly-lit reference photos of your founder as image input, mixing front, three-quarter, and profile angles, and prepend the identity-lock line above. See this pack's intro for how to build your own reference set.

**Other settings for the same mechanic:** the same calm, unhurried founder-at-home moment also works opening the door of a small home office or garden studio with coffee in hand, or sitting at a sunny café table with a closed laptop and an espresso. Swap the setting, keep the calm, unhurried posture and the phone or closed laptop as the quiet "it's handled" cue.

## Why this works

A calm, ordinary morning is the life the business is supposed to give back. Showing it plainly, no product screenshots, no dashboards, sells the outcome (freedom, calm control) instead of the mechanism.

## Audience fit

Buyers drawn to the founder's actual life and pace, not just the offer, useful for identity and lifestyle ads rather than direct-response ones.

## Adapting to your business

Keep the setting ordinary and lived-in, not staged; the whole point is that this looks like any regular morning. For The Steady Coach, this could be a quiet moment before the first call of the day. For Willow & Wren, a quiet moment before opening the shop.
