# Claymation Handoff

**Description:** A claymation owner handing an overflowing tray of work to a team of eager clay robots.

**Prompt template:**
```
Claymation still, proudly handmade look with visible fingerprints in the clay: a tired clay business owner hands a heavy overflowing paper tray labeled exactly: '[TRAY LABEL, e.g. "BOOKKEEPING," "ORDERS," "ALL OF IT"]' to a cheerful team of three small clay robots who reach up eagerly. Warm studio lighting, shallow depth of field, stop-motion film aesthetic, charming not childish. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** TRAY LABEL = "THE BUSY WORK".

## Why this works

The handoff gesture makes delegation physically visible, and handmade claymation is charming and openly artificial, which disarms rather than oversells: it looks like a bit, not a promise.

## Audience fit

Solo operators and small teams ready to let go of a specific overflowing task.

## Adapting to your business

Write the tray label as the exact burden your buyer wants off their plate. Summit Books & Tax could label the tray "RECEIPTS." Keep the handmade, fingerprints-visible claymation look and the eager robots reaching up; a slick, photoreal handoff loses the charm this style depends on.
