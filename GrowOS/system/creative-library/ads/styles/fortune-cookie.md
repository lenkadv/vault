# Fortune Cookie Promise

**Description:** A cracked fortune cookie whose paper slip carries a one-line message.

**Prompt template:**
```
Macro photoreal photo of a cracked fortune cookie on a dark cafe table beside an espresso cup. The paper fortune slip reads exactly: '[FORTUNE LINE]'. Crumbs, warm side light, extreme detail on the printed slip, shot on iPhone close-up. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** FORTUNE LINE = "Your next order is already packed."

## Why this works

A tiny, familiar object delivering a surprising promise earns a second look through sheer incongruity, and the macro close-up keeps the whole idea to one crisp line, which is exactly the amount of information a scrolling thumb will actually stop for.

## Audience fit

Broad small-business audiences who respond to a light, delightful moment rather than a hard pitch.

## Adapting to your business

Rewrite the slip to your single strongest one-line promise. Willow & Wren might use "Your order ships today," Summit Books & Tax might use "Your return is already filed." Keep the fortune-cookie framing and the one-line limit; cramming in more copy kills the joke.
