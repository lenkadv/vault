# Fashion Low-Angle Reveal

**Description:** A low-angle fashion-campaign shot with your founder holding an oversized prop close to the camera. Confident gaze, editorial lighting.

**Prompt template:**
```
[YOUR FOUNDER PHOTO, physical description: age range, hair, distinguishing features, typical clothing], shot from a low angle in a fashion-campaign style, holding a large [PROP, open laptop / phone / printed report / oversized notebook / book] close to the camera with exaggerated perspective. The [PROP] dominates the lower third of the frame. Confident, slightly serious gaze, direct eye contact. Wearing a casual [black t-shirt / dark gray henley / hoodie]. Background is a clean studio backdrop in [YOUR BRAND'S NEUTRAL OR ACCENT COLOR]. [IDENTITY-LOCK LINE, one sentence telling the model to keep your founder's exact facial structure, bone structure, nose shape, jawline, and skin texture identical across every generation, so they read as the same real person in every ad]. Editorial fashion lighting, subtle warm color grade. No watermarks. Single person only.
```

**Variations:** Prop (laptop, phone, book, clipboard), background tone, expression intensity, angle (knee-level vs floor-level).

## Why this works

The low angle and exaggerated perspective make the prop feel important and the founder feel confident, which is the visual shorthand for "I stand behind this." Direct eye contact adds a dare-you-to-look-away pull that works well as a scroll-stopper.

## Audience fit

Buyers who want a bold, confident reveal moment, good for launches or "here's the thing I built" announcements rather than quiet, pain-first ads.

## Adapting to your business

Swap the prop to whatever the buyer should associate with the offer: a printed guide, a product box, a tablet showing the work. Keep the low angle and the direct gaze; a straight-on, neutral shot loses the confident energy this style is built to deliver.
