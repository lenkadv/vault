# Glass Wall Flowchart

**Description:** A hand-drawn marker flowchart on an office glass wall, three boxes with arrows, a hand still holding the marker.

**Prompt template:**
```
Candid iPhone photo, slightly imperfect framing and mixed office lighting: a hand-drawn flowchart in black marker on an office glass wall, mild glare and reflections, a colleague's desk cluttered in the soft-blurred background. Three marker boxes connected by arrows, handwritten, reading exactly: '[BOX 1]' then '[BOX 2]' then '[BOX 3]'. A hand with the marker exits frame. Realistic imperfection, NOT a polished stock photo.
```

**Example fill:** BOX 1 = "the mess"; BOX 2 = "the system"; BOX 3 = "it ships".

## Why this works

A hand-drawn flow on real glass reads as someone actually explaining how their business works, not a designed infographic, and three boxes is exactly enough steps for a viewer to absorb the logic in one glance.

## Audience fit

Buyers who want to see the mechanism before they trust the offer, useful for "how this actually works" posts and mid-funnel explainer ads.

## Adapting to your business

Write the three boxes as your real input, process, and output. Summit Books & Tax could write "receipts" then "the system" then "filed." Keep the imperfect framing and the hand still in shot exiting frame; a perfectly centered, hand-free version reads as designed rather than caught in the act.
