# Vintage Appliance Ad

**Description:** A 1950s-style magazine-ad illustration of a cheerful appliance shaped like a filing cabinet, headline plus starburst price badge.

**Prompt template:**
```
1950s American magazine-advertisement illustration, screen-print texture and halftone dots, proudly retro: a smiling couple presents a large cheerful appliance shaped like a filing cabinet with a friendly dial face. Headline in vintage script reads exactly: '[VINTAGE HEADLINE]' Small starburst badge reads exactly: '[PRICE BADGE]' Mid-century color palette, obviously a loving pastiche. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** VINTAGE HEADLINE = "The Whole Department... in a Box!"; PRICE BADGE = "[YOUR REAL PRICE], once!" (use your actual price, never invent one).

## Why this works

Pairing an old-fashioned ad style with a modern offer is an easy, disarming pattern interrupt: the nostalgia signals "this is a bit," which makes the audience relax and actually read the joke instead of bracing for a pitch.

## Audience fit

Broad small-business audiences who appreciate humor and a clear, stated price.

## Adapting to your business

Swap the product name and use your real price on the badge, never a placeholder number left unedited. Keep the mid-century illustration style and the starburst badge; a modern typeface on this layout breaks the vintage joke.
