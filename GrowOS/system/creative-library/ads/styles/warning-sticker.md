# Warning Sticker Gag

**Description:** A yellow-and-black industrial hazard sticker on a laptop lid, carrying the punchline.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal macro photo of a laptop lid with a yellow-and-black warning sticker, industrial triangle icon. The sticker reads exactly: '[WARNING LINE]'. Slightly worn sticker edges, coffee shop bokeh background, shot on iPhone. No watermarks.
```

**Example fill:** WARNING LINE = "CAUTION: CONTAINS AN ENTIRE BUSINESS."

## Why this works

Hazard-sticker grammar is instantly recognizable, so borrowing it for a joke about how much one small tool or one small operation contains gets a laugh before the audience has processed it as an ad.

## Audience fit

Playful, personality-forward brands and solo operators who want a low-key humor moment on an everyday object.

## Adapting to your business

Write the warning line as a wry truth about your own setup. Summit Books & Tax could use "CAUTION: CONTAINS A FULL ACCOUNTING DEPARTMENT." Keep the industrial hazard-sticker look and the worn edges; a clean, new-looking sticker loses the deadpan realism the joke depends on.
