# The Bottleneck

**Description:** A surreal but photoreal office where a corridor of moving paperwork funnels down to a single doorway shaped exactly like one exhausted person.

**Prompt template:**
```
Photoreal surreal office scene: a wide corridor of moving paperwork, boards and envelopes funnels down to a single doorway exactly the shape of one exhausted person standing in it, papers queueing behind. Believable light, impossible premise, instantly legible: the person IS the bottleneck. No text.
```

## Why this works

Rendering an abstract feeling, "everything waits on me," as a literal, physically impossible scene makes it instantly legible without a single word, and the believable lighting keeps the impossible premise from tipping into cartoonish.

## Audience fit

Solo operators and any owner who is the single point everything in the business routes through, works cold since it needs no product context to land.

## Adapting to your business

Change what the corridor is made of to your audience's real deliverables: a coach's session notes, a shop's order slips, a firm's client files. Keep the "the person IS the bottleneck" premise and the no-text rule; adding a headline on top of this image competes with the metaphor instead of supporting it.
