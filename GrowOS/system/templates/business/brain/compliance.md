# Compliance

> This is what your business is not allowed to say, and what it must say. Some
> trades have rules — health, money, law, food, anything sold to children — and
> some businesses just have promises they have made. Whatever you write here, the
> system reads before it writes, and works within.
>
> **Please answer the first question even if the answer is no.** A file nobody has
> touched and a business with genuinely no rules look identical from the outside,
> and only one of them is safe to assume.

> **What is not built yet.** A single gate that checks every draft against this
> file, on every channel, is coming and is not here today. Right now this file is
> read by the skills that write, and by the reviewer pass before work reaches you —
> which is real, but it is not the same as a check that cannot be skipped. If your
> trade is genuinely regulated, keep reading the work yourself until that lands.
