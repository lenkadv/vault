# Samples

> This folder holds your own published work: posts, emails, pages, scripts —
> anything you have actually put out into the world. It is the single most useful
> thing you can give GrowOS, because your voice is easier to copy than to describe.
> This README explains the folder; you do not fill it in.

## Why this matters more than it sounds

`voice.md` describes how you sound. This folder *shows* it. Three real emails
teach the system more about your rhythm, your sentence length, and the jokes you
actually make than a page of adjectives ever will.

It is also the honest source for "write me another one like that". Without
samples, the system is guessing at your style from a description of it.

## What to put here

- Posts that did well, and posts that felt like you even if they did nothing.
- Emails and newsletters you actually sent.
- Landing page or sales page copy.
- Video or podcast scripts, or a transcript if that is what exists.
- Anything you were happy to have your name on.

One file per piece. Paste the text as it went out — not a tidied-up version. The
mess is the signal.

## How to name them

A short name that says what it is: `email-welcome.md`, `post-why-we-raised-prices.md`,
`page-home.md`. Add a line at the top saying where it ran and roughly when, if you
remember. If you do not, add it anyway — an undated sample still teaches voice.

## The rules

- **Yours only.** Only your own published material goes here. Someone else's
  writing in this folder would teach the system to sound like them.
- **As published.** Do not clean it up first. GrowOS learns from what you really
  wrote, including the bits you would edit now.
- **Not proof.** A customer's kind words are proof and live in `proof/`. This
  folder is your writing, not your results.
- **Nothing published from here.** These are already out in the world. The system
  reads them to learn; it never re-posts them.

Five good samples beat fifty. Start with the three pieces you would show someone
who asked what you do.
