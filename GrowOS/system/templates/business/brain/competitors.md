# Competitors

> This is who else your customer could pick, and what they say when they do. The
> system uses it to keep your work sounding like you instead of like everyone else
> in your market, and to find the angle nobody is taking. It fills in what it can
> find during setup; correct anything that is off.

_Research can add lines here too, each with a mark: **confirmed** (you checked
it), **rough** (close enough, not exact), or **unchecked** (research found it,
nobody has confirmed it yet). Anything unmarked counts as unchecked._

---

_Note the scope on purpose. You looked at two or three competitors, so what you
found is "different from the ones I checked" — not "the only one in the world who
does this". GrowOS writes it the narrower way, because the wider claim is one you
cannot back and the kind a customer enjoys disproving._

_Keep this to what you can stand behind. GrowOS never invents a competitor's
price, claim, or failing, and never writes a comparison it cannot support from
what is written here._
