## House style overrides

GrowOS writes in grade-8 plain words by default. That is right for most
businesses and wrong for some — a paper for surgeons or an API guide for
developers should not read like a leaflet. Set anything here and it wins **for
work written for your audience**.

What it does not change: how GrowOS talks to *you*. Its questions, explanations
and health reports stay in plain words whatever you set here, because those are a
tool talking to its owner, not marketing. The charter and the skill standard both
draw the line the same way.

Leave a line blank and the default stands.

- **Jargon that is fine here:** [PLACEHOLDER: terms your reader already knows and does not need explained]
- **Anything else about how it should read:** [PLACEHOLDER: length, formality, whether contractions are fine]

**What cannot be overridden.** These are not style, and nothing written here
changes them: never inventing a fact, quote, or result; never publishing without
your approval; the compliance rules in `brain/compliance.md`; and keeping secrets
out of everything. A style note can change how the work sounds. It can never
change what the system is allowed to do.
