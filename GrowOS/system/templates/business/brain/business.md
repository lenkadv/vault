## The hard facts (never contradict these)
These are the true, checkable facts about your business. The system reads them
before it writes anything and works from them rather than making numbers up.

Mark each one so the system knows how far it can lean on it:

- **confirmed** — you have checked it and would put it in writing.
- **rough** — near enough for you, but not a number to publish as fact.
- **unchecked** — someone said it once. Useful context, never a public claim.

Anything not marked is treated as unchecked.

- [PLACEHOLDER: a real number, claim, or fact you can stand behind] — **[PLACEHOLDER: confirmed / rough / unchecked]**
- [PLACEHOLDER: another hard fact, or delete this line] — **[PLACEHOLDER: confirmed / rough / unchecked]**

_Worth being straight about: this whole section is **guidance, not enforcement**.
No code compares a draft against these facts. The system reads them and writes
from them, and the reviewer gate and your own approval are what catch a slip — but
nothing will refuse a draft that contradicts a line above, and nothing will stop
an unchecked number being published if you approve it. Marking a fact
**unchecked** is how you tell the system not to reach for it; it is not a lock._
