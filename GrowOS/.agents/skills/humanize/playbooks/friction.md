# Playbook: friction

Read the piece the way its real reader will, and find where they stall, doubt, or
leave. Run inside the SKILL.md method. This playbook is judgment work; the scorer
does not help here.

## How to read

Top to bottom, in reading order, once, as the audience described in
`brain/audience.md` - their situation, their scepticism, their attention span.
Not a generic reader. Note the exact line where each snag happens; every finding
quotes the text it is about.

## The snags to catch

- **Confusion.** A sentence you had to read twice; a term the audience does not
  use; a pronoun with no clear owner; steps out of order.
- **Doubt.** A claim with nothing behind it where proof exists in `brain/proof/`
  (approved entries only), or a claim so big it needs softening or a placeholder.
  Never patch doubt by inventing evidence (E01).
- **Overwhelm.** Three offers in one email. Six ideas in one post. Choices the
  reader was not ready to make. One piece, one job.
- **The ask.** Is there a call to action? Is it one thing? Can the reader do it in
  the next minute? Is it buried under a sign-off or hidden mid-paragraph? Weak,
  missing, or buried asks are the most common friction finding.
- **Price shock.** If a price appears, does anything before it carry the weight?
  Flag a naked number with nothing around it.
- **Unanswered objection.** The question the reader is guaranteed to ask ("do I
  need to know code?", "what if it does not fit?") that the piece never touches.
- **Logic holes.** A promise the body never delivers; a "therefore" that does not
  follow; a step that assumes something the reader was never told.

## Fixing

Smallest change that removes the snag: reorder before rewrite, cut before add.
Additions obey the fact-freeze. Do not add reassurance, proof, or specifics from
the brain. Reorder or clarify what the source already says; when something truly
is missing, use a `[PLACEHOLDER - ...]` for the owner. And stay conservative: this
playbook finds real snags, it does not hunt for them. A clean section gets a
clean pass, not an invented note.
