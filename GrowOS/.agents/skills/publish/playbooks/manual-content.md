# Manual content handoff

Use this when no safe connection exists or a thread or unsupported media package
cannot be preserved.

1. Give the owner the approved text, media list, destination, and exact approved
   schedule when one exists. Do not add or rewrite copy.
2. While status stays approved, record the manual destination, exact attempt time,
   `publish_state: waiting-owner`, and what confirmation is needed.
3. Wait. Showing the package is not completion.
4. After the owner confirms the manual post, draft, or schedule, change the receipt
   to `prepared`, record the confirmation and exact completion time, then make the
   separate move to published.

If the owner reports a mismatch, keep the item approved and set `blocked` with
their reason. Do not claim the manual action succeeded.
