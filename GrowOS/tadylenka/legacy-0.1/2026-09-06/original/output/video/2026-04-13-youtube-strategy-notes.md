# YouTube Strategy Notes

**Date:** 2026-04-13
**Status:** Pre-launch planning

---

## Format Decision

Long-form video about art history popularization. Target length: 15-20 min.

---

## Structural Template (derived from Great Art Explained analysis)

1. **Dramatic hook** (30 sec) — paradox, contrast, surprising fact
2. **Roadmap** (30 sec) — "V tomhle videu se podíváme na..."
3. **Biography + historical context woven together** (5-7 min)
4. **Deep visual analysis of the artwork** (5-7 min)
5. **Zoom-out to universal meaning / contemporary relevance** (2-3 min)
6. **Poetic conclusion** (30 sec)

---

## Voice Principles for Video

- Tongue-in-cheek, not slapstick. Wit, not jokes.
- Authoritative without lecturing. Like talking to an intelligent friend.
- Wide context: not just art-to-art, but art-to-everything (politics, literature, science, daily life of the era).
- Contemporary relevance is mandatory. Always answer: "Why does this matter now?"
- No dumbing down. Trust the audience. Give them context, not simplified versions.
- Deadpan delivery of absurd historical facts (brand voice strength — use it).

---

## Key Differentiators vs. Great Art Explained

| Great Art Explained | tadylenka |
|---|---|
| English, global audience | Czech, niche but underserved market |
| Earnest, cinematic tone | Deadpan literary, tongue-in-cheek |
| Focused on single artwork | Can go wider: movements, themes, connections |
| Artist-to-artist context | Artist-to-everything: politics, literature, music, philosophy |
| "This is extraordinary" | Absurd situations presented as mundane facts |
