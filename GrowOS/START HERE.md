# Welcome to GrowOS

This is your marketing team in a folder.

You bring the business. GrowOS does the marketing work, and everything it makes
waits for your yes before it goes anywhere. Nothing is ever sent or posted
without you. If you only remember one thing, remember that.

## What GrowOS is

Think of it as a smart assistant that already knows how to run marketing. It
writes your posts, emails, and ideas, keeps track of your business, and hands
each piece to you to approve. You stay the boss. It does the heavy lifting.

There is no app to log into and no dashboard to learn. It all lives right here,
in plain files you own.

## Start in three steps

1. **Open this folder in the Claude Code desktop app.** That is the app that
   brings GrowOS to life. If you do not have it yet, install it first, then use
   its "open folder" option and pick this folder.

2. **Just say hi.** Type "hi" in the chat and press enter. That is really all it
   takes. GrowOS will greet you and offer to get set up.

3. **Answer a few easy questions.** It will ask for your website, or anything you
   already have, like your Instagram or a PDF about your business. From that it
   builds a first draft of who you are, and asks you to correct anything wrong.
   Correcting is easy. You will know your own business when you see it.

That is the whole start. Ten minutes, tops.

## What happens next

Once you are set up, you can ask for things in normal words:

- "Write me a post about our new offer."
- "What should I work on this week?"
- "Show me what is waiting for me."

Every draft lands in one place called your **review queue**. When you have a
minute, you look it over, and you say "approved" or "change this." Nothing goes
anywhere before your yes. What happens AFTER your yes is your choice, per
channel: out of the box, approved work is handed to you in a safe state — an
unsent draft in your email tool, a paused ad, a private upload — and you press
the last button. If you later choose `live` for a channel (in that business's
`setup.md`), approved work on it goes out for real, confirmed with you first,
in the session. Support replies are the exception: those are always drafts you
send yourself.

## Where to learn more

There is no manual to read, on purpose. Ask GrowOS instead — it can explain any
part of itself in plain words, and it is always looking at your actual folder
rather than a page written months ago.

Things worth asking:

- "Walk me through my first day."
- "What does a normal week look like?"
- "How does the review queue work?"
- "Something feels off — run the doctor." (It checks itself and tells you what
  it found, in plain words, with a fix for each thing.)

Welcome aboard. Say hi when you are ready.
