5 otázek k rozbití slabých argumentů (filosofie / debata / rétorika).

Here are five devastating questioning techniques Socrates developed that can destroy any weak argument through pure logic. 
First, **what exactly do you mean by that**? This forces people to define their terms clearly. Most people realize they don't actually understand their own position when pressed for specifics. 
Second, **how do you know that's true**? This exposes the weak foundations most beliefs are built on. People often discover their certainty comes from assumptions, not facts. 
Third, **what evidence would change your mind**? This reveals true intellectual honesty versus stubborn dogmatism. Those who can't answer this question aren't thinking, they're just defending. 
Fourth, **what are the implications of your wrong**? This forces consideration of real consequences. It makes people examine the actual cost of their beliefs and decisions. 
Fifth, his most powerful weapon, **why is this important to you**? This reveals the emotional attachments driving supposedly logical thinking. Once you see the emotion behind the argument, the whole structure often crumbles. Master these questions, and you'll think clearer than 90% of people.
