**Spuštění:** /carousel-create (automaticky v sezení otevřeném v GrowOS; z vaultu až po otevření souboru v GrowOS)

**Popis od autora:** Build an Instagram or LinkedIn carousel: pick the slide arc, write the per-slide copy, build the per-slide design against brand.md, and either render every slide or hand over a per-slide design brief - never fakes a render it cannot do. Triggers: "make a carousel", "turn this into a carousel", "carousel for instagram", "linkedin carousel", "swipe post", "carousel about X". Not for a single image (image-create), the caption or posting copy that goes with the carousel (social-write drafts that, and references this asset), or a carousel built for a paid ad (ads-meta-create owns that carousel format).
