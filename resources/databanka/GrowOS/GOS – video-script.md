# video-script

**Použij, až:** Write a video script — YouTube long-form, a short-form reel or short, a talking-head piece, or UGC-style native content — from a topic through research, a…

**Spuštění:** /video-script (automaticky v sezení otevřeném v GrowOS; z vaultu až po otevření souboru v GrowOS)

**Popis od autora:** Write a video script — YouTube long-form, a short-form reel or short, a talking-head piece, or UGC-style native content — from a topic through research, a structure map, an annotated master script with delivery cues, and a teleprompter-ready cut plus production brief. Reads the brain for the spoken voice register, the audience's own words, methodology, stories, and proof, then drafts whichever of the four types the ask calls for, before the Editor gate and the queue. Triggers: "write a video script", "youtube script", "script for a reel", "short-form video script", "talking head script", "UGC video script", "write my youtube video". Does not write a sales video script (that's vsl-write's job) or ad-round video creative (ads-meta-create's job), and does not film, edit, caption, build the YouTube upload package, or make thumbnails.
