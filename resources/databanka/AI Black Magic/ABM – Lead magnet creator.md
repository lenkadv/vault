# Lead magnet creator

**Použij, až:** tvoříš lead magnet

Creates lead magnet content (checklists, cheat sheets, mini-guides, templates) plus the landing page copy to promote it, including headline, benefit bullets, CTA, and objection handling. Use when a user wants to grow their email list, needs a free downloadable resource, or wants to create an opt-in incentive for their audience.
